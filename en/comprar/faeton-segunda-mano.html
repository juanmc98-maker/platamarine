Used Faeton for sale

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

Faeton is a Spanish yard best known for its Moraga series: wheelhouse boats for fishing and cruising, with a hull designed to handle a sea. They have a strong following in the Balearics and along the Mediterranean coast, and people search for them by name.

About the range

The Moragas (for example the 730 and 780) combine a wheelhouse, a cabin with a berth and a large fishing cockpit. Many have an inboard diesel with sterndrive, which is economical and lasts many hours if well maintained.

What to check on a used Faeton

  • Diesel and drive: hours, service invoices and condition of bellows and drive.
  • Wheelhouse: windows, seals and possible leaks.
  • Fishing kit: sounder, rod holders, livewell; check it works.
  • Hull: antifouling, anodes and knocks; on boats of this age, check the deck too.

Guide prices

Each boat varies a lot by year, engine and equipment, so I do not publish a general range. If you own one or are looking at one, tell me the year, engine and hours and I will tell you where it sits.

Which licence you need

Faetons up to 8 metres need a PNB or higher; those over 8 metres, PER or higher.

Frequently asked questions

Why are Faetons so popular?

The hull, which handles a sea well, and the wheelhouse, which lets you go out on days you would not in an open boat.

Which licence do I need for a Faeton Moraga 730?

At 7.39 metres, a PNB or higher is enough.
