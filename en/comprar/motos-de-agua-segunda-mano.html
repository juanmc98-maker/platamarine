Buy · Jet skis

Used jet skis

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

A used jet ski can be a great buy or a headache, and the difference is almost always in what the photos do not show: how it has been used and looked after. Here is what I check before recommending one and the paperwork you need in Spain.

What to check before buying

  • Engine hours: not everything, but they matter. Also ask how it has been used: a private owner's holidays are not the same as a rental fleet.
  • Maintenance: invoices for the yearly service (oil, plugs, anodes, filters) and whether it was flushed with fresh water after every outing, especially the cooling circuit.
  • Jet pump and impeller: damage or wear from stones and sand. A damaged pump shows in acceleration and vibration.
  • Hull: knocks, cracks or repairs underneath, from beaching or the trailer.
  • Engine: it should start cold without trouble and not overheat. If it is supercharged, ask when that was serviced.
  • Trailer: lights, rollers and paperwork in order.

If the jet ski is worth it, an inspection by a PWC workshop before paying is cheap compared with a breakdown. The general checklist is in what to check when buying used.

Which licence you need

In Spain you need a licence to ride a jet ski: the Licencia de Navegación or higher. With the Licencia you can ride it in daylight and within 2 miles of a port or shelter. The PNB also covers it, within the jet ski's own limits. See boating licences.

In summer, use the buoyed lanes to leave and reach the beach and keep clear of swimming areas: that is where most fines are given.

Paperwork and what you pay when buying

  • Check it is registered to the seller and free of charges.
  • Third-party liability insurance is compulsory.
  • Buying from a private seller, you pay your region's transfer tax (ITP; 5% in Catalonia). See the ITP guide by region.
  • Then the change of ownership. More in papers and change of ownership.

What affects the price

Brand and model, power, year, hours and, above all, condition and invoices. A recent jet ski with many rental hours can be worth less than an older, well-kept private one. Tell me the model, year and hours and I will tell you whether a price is in line with the market.

Frequently asked questions

Can I ride a jet ski in Spain without a licence?

No. Royal Decree 875/2014 requires a licence to ride personal watercraft: at least the Licencia de Navegación.

Which licence do I need for a jet ski in Spain?

The Licencia de Navegación or higher. With the Licencia, in daylight and within 2 miles of a port or shelter. The PNB also covers jet skis, within their technical limits.

How many hours is a lot for a jet ski?

It depends on the model and, above all, maintenance. Rather than the number, look at the invoices and how it has been used: rental jet skis rack up hours quickly.
