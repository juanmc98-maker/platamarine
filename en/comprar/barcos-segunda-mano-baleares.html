If you will use the boat on the islands

The Balearics are the part of Spain where most boats are registered, and demand for berths is very high in summer. If the boat is sold with a berth or a dry-stack space, ask about it first: it can matter as much as the boat itself.

If you want to take it to the mainland

There are two options: under its own power, or by road and ferry. To cross under way you need the PER with passage training (or a higher licence) and a boat prepared for the crossing. For small boats, transport is usually the sensible choice; if needed, I can help you arrange it.

Taxes depend on where you live

ITP depends on the region where the buyer lives, not where the boat is. If you live in Catalonia and buy a boat in Mallorca from a private seller, you pay the Catalan 5%. If you live in another region, check the rate with your gestoría. Run the numbers with the tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a boat in Mallorca if I live on the mainland?

Yes. The purchase and change of ownership work the same way; what changes is how you bring the boat over and the ITP, which depends on your region of residence.

What licence do I need to cross from the Balearics to the mainland?

The PER with passage training, or a higher licence. The PNB or the Navigation Licence are not enough.

Can I see the boat properly before flying to the islands?

Before you travel I ask for specific photos, engine hours, papers and whatever else is needed, so the trip is not wasted.
