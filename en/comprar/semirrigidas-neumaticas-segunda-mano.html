Used RIBs and inflatable boats for sale

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

RIBs are among the boats that sell best second-hand: light, stable, shallow draught and easy to keep ashore. These are the ones I have now and what I always check before recommending one.

RIB or inflatable: the difference

An inflatable is fully inflatable, with a slatted, inflatable or wooden floor: light, folds up and fits in a car boot, but handles worse in a sea. A RIB (rigid inflatable boat) has a rigid GRP or aluminium hull with tubes around it: it handles much better, takes a bigger engine and is the one used for trips to coves or as a tender for a large boat.

In Spain many people call them "zodiac" after the brand, but there are many builders: Zodiac, Bombard, Capelli, Joker Boat, BSC, Lomac, Sacs and Tiger Marine, among others.

What to check on the tubes

  • Material: PVC or Hypalon (CSM). Hypalon usually stands up better to the sun and lasts longer; PVC is cheaper. Ask which one it has and how old the tubes are.
  • Airtightness: ask for them to be inflated the day before. If they have lost pressure, valves or seams are leaking.
  • Bonding: areas coming unstuck, patches and the joint between tube and hull.
  • Appearance: a very faded, sticky or cracked tube is at the end of its life.

Replacing the tubes on a 6-metre RIB is a major cost, several thousand euros: factor it into the price.

Hull, console and engine

  • Hull: keel damage from groundings or the trailer, and a solid transom around the engine bolts.
  • Outboard: hours, invoices and annual service (oils, impeller, anodes).
  • Trailer: lights, rollers and paperwork.

More in what to check when buying a used boat.

Which licence you need

Up to 6 metres on the papers, the Licencia de Navegación, as long as the engine is suitable for the boat according to the builder. A 6.5-metre RIB already needs the PNB. That is why 6-metre RIBs are so sought after. More in boating licences.

How much it costs

On a RIB the tubes and the engine matter more than the hull year. As a guide, the two I currently have, of 5.35 and 6.50 metres with 115 and 150 hp engines, are listed between €25,000 and €33,000 (October 2026). A small used inflatable costs much less.

Frequently asked questions

How long do RIB tubes last?

It depends on the material, the sun and how the boat has been stored. As a rough guide, PVC lasts less than Hypalon, and a boat kept under cover or with a cover keeps them much better. If they are the original tubes on an old boat, budget for replacing them.

Which licence do I need for a 6-metre RIB?

With 6 metres or less on the papers, the Licencia de Navegación, as long as the engine is suitable according to the builder. If longer, the PNB.

Does a RIB need the ITB inspection?

If it is on list 7 and its hull is under 6 metres, it has no periodic inspections. At 6 metres or more, at least every 5 years. I explain it in the ITB guide.
