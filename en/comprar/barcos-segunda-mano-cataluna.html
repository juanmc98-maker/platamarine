Along the coast

The same boat is bought and used very differently depending on the area. On the Costa Brava it is all about cove-hopping boats: sundeck, walkaround and open, and berths are scarce in summer. Barcelona and the Maresme have large marinas and plenty of supply, but rented berths are among the most expensive. In the Garraf, Costa Daurada and Terres de l'Ebre you see more fishing boats and cabin cruisers. Before buying, check what a berth will cost where you want it: rates for 19 marinas are in the Catalonia berth guide.

On the listings I only give the general area of the boat, not the exact marina.

Taxes if you buy in Catalonia

If you live in Catalonia and buy from a private seller, you pay 5% ITP on the price, or on the tax office table value if higher, within one month. If the boat comes from abroad and is over 8 metres, there is also the 12% registration tax. Run the numbers with the tax calculator.

Which licence you need

  • Navigation Licence (Licencia de Navegación): boats up to 6 metres, in daylight and close to the coast.
  • PNB: up to 8 metres and 5 miles from a shelter.
  • PER: up to 15 metres and 12 miles; with the passage training, up to 24 metres and crossing to the Balearics. For sailboats, the sailing practice as well.

It is all explained in boating licences.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used boat cost in Catalonia?

It depends on type and length: a 6 to 7 metre open boat goes for roughly €20,000 to €60,000, and a 7 to 8.5 metre sundeck or walkaround from €25,000 to over €100,000 depending on the year. Ranges by model are on the prices page.

What tax do you pay when buying a used boat in Catalonia?

5% ITP if you buy from a private seller, on the price or the tax office table value if higher. If you buy from a company, the sale carries VAT instead of ITP.

Is it hard to find a berth in Catalonia?

In summer and on the Costa Brava, yes. If the boat is sold with a transferable berth, it is worth factoring into the price.

Where can I buy a used boat in Catalonia?

There are listing portals, dealers selling their own stock and brokers like me, who work with a mandate signed by the owner. Wherever you buy, always ask for the registry extract (hoja de asiento) to check there are no charges, review the paperwork and, if the boat warrants it, get a survey before you pay. What to check when buying a used boat.

What paperwork do I need to buy a used boat in Catalonia?

A complete sale contract, an up-to-date registry extract, the registration certificate, the navigation permit and a valid ITB inspection. Afterwards you have one month to pay the transfer tax (ITP) and three months to register the change of ownership with the Harbour Master. Paperwork and change of ownership.
