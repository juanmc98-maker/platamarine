What each licence allows

  • Navigation Licence (Licencia de Navegación): boats up to 6 metres, in daylight and close to the coast.
  • PNB: up to 8 metres and 5 miles from a shelter.
  • PER: up to 15 metres and 12 miles; with the passage training, up to 24 metres and crossing to the Balearics. For sailboats, the sailing practice as well.

It is all explained in boating licences.

Which boats fit

With the Navigation Licence, open boats and RIBs up to 6 metres. With the PNB the choice widens to sundeck, walkaround and pilothouse boats up to 8 metres, which are among the best-selling used boats in Catalonia. On each listing I give the minimum licence as "or higher", always confirmed against the boat's papers.

If you plan to get the PER

If in a few years you will want a bigger boat or to cross to the Balearics, it is worth thinking about the PER now: a 7 to 8 metre boat bought with the PNB still sells well when you want to move up. You can practise with the PER practice test.

Frequently asked questions

What boat can I skipper with the Navigation Licence?

Boats up to 6 metres long, in daylight and close to the coast. It is the licence to start with a small open boat or a RIB.

What boat can I skipper with the PNB?

Motorboats up to 8 metres, up to 5 miles from a shelter. For sailboats, with the sailing practice.

Is the length in the listing the one that counts?

What counts is the length in the boat's papers. That is why on each listing the licence is confirmed against the documents.
