By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

Quicksilver Activ boats are Mercury outboard runabouts built for hassle-free days on the water. They are among the best-selling boats of their size, so there is plenty of choice and you need to choose well.

About the range

There are Open (centre console), Sundeck (cuddy under the bow), Cruiser and Pilothouse versions, from about 5 to 9 metres. Boat and engine come from the same group, which usually makes servicing and warranty simpler.

What to check on a used Quicksilver Activ

  • Mercury engine: hours, dealer servicing and any remaining warranty (sometimes extendable).
  • Upholstery and covers: condition of cushions and canopy.
  • Trailer: if included, it should be legal and in the seller's name.
  • Version and extras: two Activs of the same year can differ a lot in price because of engine and equipment.

Guide prices

Published ranges: Activ 605 Open, 2016 to 2021 €20,000 to €27,000, 2022 to 2023 €24,000 to €36,000 and 2024 onwards €33,000 to €48,000; Activ 675 Sundeck, 2011 to 2016 €25,000 to €32,000, 2017 to 2020 €36,000 to €45,000 and 2021 onwards €45,000 to €55,000. Model guides with more detail: Activ 605 Open, Activ 675 Sundeck.

Which licence you need

Up to 6 metres, Navigation Licence or higher if the power allows; 6 to 8 metres, PNB or higher.

Frequently asked questions

Open or Sundeck?

The Open gives more room to move and fish; the Sundeck adds a cabin for storage and shelter. For families with children, the Sundeck is usually more practical.

Which engine size is best?

One that gets the loaded boat on the plane without running at its limit. An underpowered engine works harder and burns more.
