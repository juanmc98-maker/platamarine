Used Jeanneau Merry Fisher for sale

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

The Merry Fisher is Jeanneau's fishing and cruising range with a cabin: wheelhouse, side decks to move around safely and a cabin to sleep in. It is the Antares' main rival and one of the most sought-after boats of its type.

About the range

Current Merry Fishers run outboards and range from about 6 to 11 metres (695, 795, 895, 1095...). Older ones often have an inboard. The 795 is probably the most popular: it fits an 8-metre berth and has a proper cabin.

What to check on a used Jeanneau Merry Fisher

  • Engine: hours, year and servicing; on outboards, whether it was under warranty.
  • Wheelhouse and doors: seals, sliding doors and leaks.
  • Side decks and cockpit: non-slip and drains, which suffer from fishing use.
  • Series: recent versions change quite a lot; compare within the same series.

Guide prices

For the Merry Fisher 795 I have published ranges: 2016 to 2017, €45,000 to €72,000 (wide spread depending on engine and equipment); 2018 to 2021, €64,000 to €83,000; 2022 onwards, €87,000 to €105,000. Model guides with more detail: Merry Fisher 795, Merry Fisher 895.

Which licence you need

Up to 8 metres, PNB or higher. The 895 has a 7.98 m hull according to Jeanneau, so a PNB should normally be enough; check the length on the boat's papers. Larger ones, such as the 1095, need a PER or higher.

Frequently asked questions

Merry Fisher or Antares?

They are very similar in concept. Judge each boat on its own: engine, hours, equipment and price matter more than the brand.

Which Merry Fisher is best for sleeping aboard?

From the 795 up there is a comfortable cabin for a couple; for a family, the 895 or larger.
