How buying with me works

  1. You choose the boat here, or tell me what you are after.
  2. I ask for whatever is missing before you travel: specific photos, engine hours, ITB inspection, invoices and papers.
  3. Viewing and sea trial, where I come along when there is a serious buyer.
  4. Survey: I always recommend an independent inspection before closing.
  5. Contract and paperwork: sale contract, ITP and change of ownership through a nautical gestoría.

If the boat you buy is one I have for sale, I charge you nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy a used boat in Spain with guarantees?

From someone who stands behind the listing. Every boat I list has a mandate signed by its owner; before you travel I ask for whatever is missing (papers, hours, invoices) and I recommend an independent survey before closing. The mandate does not certify the boat's condition: that is what the survey is for.

Do I pay a fee if I buy a Plata Marine boat?

No. If the boat is one I have for sale, the seller pays the fee.

What papers should a used boat have in Spain?

At least the registration in the seller's name, the seaworthiness certificate with a valid ITB if applicable, and proof that taxes were paid. It is detailed in the guide on what to check when buying.
