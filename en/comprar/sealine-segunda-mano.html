About the range

The brand ran into trouble in 2013 and was taken over by the HanseYachts group, which still builds it. Most used Sealines in Spain predate that change and have inboard engines (often twins) on shafts or sterndrives.

What to check on a used Sealine

  • Engines: with twins, the hours on each, invoices, and that both start and pull evenly.
  • Drives or shafts: bellows, oil, propellers and stern glands.
  • Interior: damp, upholstery, galley and heads; on older boats, the electrics.
  • Paperwork: length and engines on the papers should match the boat.

Guide prices

Each boat varies a lot by year, engine and equipment, so I do not publish a general range. If you own one or are looking at one, tell me the year, engine and hours and I will tell you where it sits.

Which licence you need

Most Sealines are over 8 metres: PER or higher (and above 15 metres, PER with the additional practice or Yacht Skipper).

Frequently asked questions

Are parts available for an older Sealine?

Engines, drives and electronics come from manufacturers with service networks. For boat-specific parts there are specialists and a second-hand market, but the essentials should be sound.

What does a Sealine cost to run?

It depends a lot on the engines and use. Before buying, run the numbers with the annual cost calculator.
