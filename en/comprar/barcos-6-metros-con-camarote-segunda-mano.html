Buy a boat · 6 to 7.5 metres

Used 6-metre boats with a cabin

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

It is one of the most common requests I get: an easy-to-handle boat that fits an affordable berth and has somewhere to store gear, take a nap or spend the odd night. Here are the ones I have for sale in that size and what I would check before buying one.

What "cabin" means on a 6-metre boat

At this length there is rarely a proper cabin, but rather a space in the bow with a V-berth for two adults, sometimes with a toilet. Depending on the layout, you will find:

  • Cabin cruiser: the cabin takes the bow and the cockpit is aft. The most common layout at 6 metres.
  • Pilothouse: an enclosed helm. Ideal for fishing and year-round boating.
  • Walkaround: side decks to walk forward and a small cabin below. Very handy at anchor.
  • Sundeck with cabin: a bow sunpad with a small cabin below; more for coves than for sleeping.

If you plan to sleep aboard, get inside during the viewing: measure the berth, check the headroom and think about ventilation. At 6 metres the differences between models are big.

Which licence you need

What counts is the length on the registration papers, not the one in the ad.

  • Up to 6 metres: Licencia de Navegación, daylight only and within 2 miles of a port or shelter.
  • Up to 8 metres: PNB, up to 5 miles.
  • To go further: PER.

Many boats advertised as "6 metres" measure 6.2 or 6.5 m on paper, and then the Licencia is no longer enough. Ask for the registry extract before going to see it. More in boating licences.

How much it costs

It depends a lot on the year, the engine and the brand. As a guide, for the 6 to 7-metre models I track every month, asking prices run from about €20,000 to €60,000, and a 7.5-metre pilothouse such as the used Merry Fisher 795 goes from €45,000 to €105,000 depending on the year. These are asking prices, not sale prices. See the ranges by model in used boat prices.

Models I would look at

With a cabin and at this length you often see the Jeanneau Merry Fisher 605 and 695, the Beneteau Antares 6 and 7 metres and, on older boats, Spanish brands such as Faeton or Rio. If you want more sunpad than cabin, look at the Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck or the Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck. And if you can stretch to just over 7 metres, the Cap Camarat 7.5 WA is one of the most sought-after walkarounds.

What to check before buying

  • Damp in the cabin: window and hatch seals, stains and a musty smell.
  • Engine: hours and invoices; on an outboard, whether it has been serviced every year.
  • Trailer: many boats of this size are kept ashore; if it has one, check its paperwork is in order.
  • Papers: length, ITB (technical inspection) and no outstanding charges.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skipper a 6-metre boat with the Licencia de Navegación?

Yes, if the length on the papers is no more than 6 metres and the engine is suitable for the boat according to the builder. Daylight only and within 2 miles of a port or shelter.

Can you sleep on a 6-metre boat?

For a weekend, two adults can, in the bow V-berth. For longer trips a boat of 7 metres or more with a toilet and a small galley is much nicer.

How much does a berth for a 6-metre boat cost?

It varies a lot by port and season. The berth guide has the rates of 19 Catalan ports, and many boats of this size are kept ashore, which can work out cheaper.
