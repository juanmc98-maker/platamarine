How much does a boat cost? Used boat prices by type and length

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

It is almost everyone's first question. The short answer: from a few thousand euros for an inflatable to hundreds of thousands for a yacht. The useful answer has three parts: what it costs to buy, what you pay when buying and what it costs to keep.

Price by type and length

These are the ranges of real listings for the 6 to 9-metre models I track every month on the used boat prices page, engine included:

TypeLengthUsual second-hand range
Centre-console open boat6 to 7 m€20,000 to €60,000
Sundeck7 to 8 m€25,000 to €92,500
Walkaround7 to 8.5 m€32,000 to €120,000
Pilothouse7.5 to 9 m€45,000 to €180,000
Cabin cruiser8 to 8.5 m€35,000 to €67,000

These are asking prices; the sale price is usually lower. Within each range, what moves the price most is the year, the engine and its hours, and whether there are invoices.

RIBs, sailboats and larger boats

To give you real references, this is what I have listed now (October 2026): two RIBs of 5.35 and 6.50 metres between €25,000 and €33,000; a 12-metre 1981 steel sailboat for €72,000 and a 2008 Beneteau Oceanis 50 for €155,000; and a 2006 Jeanneau Prestige 32 flybridge, 10.65 metres, for €89,000. A large older boat can cost less than a small brand-new one.

What you pay when buying (Spain)

  • Buying from a private seller: transfer tax (ITP), 5% in Catalonia on the price or on the tax authority's table value if higher. Each region has its own rate: see the ITP guide by region.
  • Buying from a company: VAT applies instead of ITP.
  • If the boat comes from abroad and is over 8 metres: also the 12% registration tax.
  • Paperwork: change of ownership at the Harbour Master's office and, if you use them, an agency and a survey.

Work it out with the tax calculator.

What it costs to keep each year

  • Berth or dry stack: usually the biggest cost. Rates for 19 ports in the berth guide.
  • Insurance: third-party liability is compulsory.
  • Maintenance: haul-out, antifouling, engine service and small repairs.
  • ITB: depending on length and list.
  • Fuel: depends on how much you go out and the engine.

Work out your case with the yearly cost calculator or read how much it costs to keep a boat in Catalonia.

How to tell if a price is good

Compare it with listings of the same model and year, ask for invoices and ask how long it has been for sale. There are ranges by year on the model pages, such as the Merry Fisher 795, the Cap Camarat 7.5 WA or the Cap Camarat 8.5 WA, and the full method in how to price a used boat.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a small boat cost?

A used inflatable or small boat can be found for a few thousand euros. A 6 to 7-metre open boat in good condition is between about €20,000 and €60,000.

How much does a yacht cost?

It depends on what you call a yacht. A 10 to 12-metre flybridge cruiser a few years old can be around €100,000, and yachts of 15 metres or more run into hundreds of thousands or millions.

How much does it cost to keep a boat each year?

It depends mainly on the berth, the length and how much you use it. The yearly cost calculator lets you work out your own case.
