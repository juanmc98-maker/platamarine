Used Beneteau Flyer for sale

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

The Flyer is Beneteau's day-boat range: swim, anchor in a cove and head back. Outboard power, lots of deck and sunpads. It is an easy boat to sell and resell, which is why it is so sought after.

About the range

You will mostly see three versions: Open (centre console, maximum deck), SUNdeck (with a small cuddy under the bow) and SPACEdeck (large bow sunpad and side console). Lengths run from about 5 to 10 metres; 6 to 8 metres is where most supply and demand is.

What to check on a used Beneteau Flyer

  • Outboard: hours, year, dealer servicing and any remaining warranty.
  • Upholstery and sunpads: the sun is hard on them; replacing cushions is a cost to factor in.
  • Trailer: if included, it should be in the seller's name with its roadworthiness test up to date.
  • Charter use: ask whether it has been chartered; it shows in hours and wear.

Guide prices

Published ranges: Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck, 2015 to 2017 €26,000 to €36,500 and 2018 to 2020 €29,000 to €43,000; Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck, 2015 to 2016 €41,000 to €59,000 and 2017 to 2018 €54,000 to €72,500. Model guides with more detail: Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck, Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck, Flyer 8 SUNdeck.

Which licence you need

Flyers up to 6 metres can be run on a Navigation Licence or higher if the power allows; 6 to 8 metres, PNB or higher; above that, PER or higher.

Frequently asked questions

SUNdeck or SPACEdeck?

The SUNdeck has a cabin for storage or a nap; the SPACEdeck gains sunpad and bow space. If you will ever sleep aboard, SUNdeck; if it is day use only, SPACEdeck.

Does a Flyer resell well?

It is one of the most in-demand used boats in the Mediterranean, so it usually sells well if it is priced right and well kept.
