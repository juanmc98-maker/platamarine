What to check on a used sailboat

  • Rigging: wires, terminals and chainplates. If it has not been replaced for many years, budget for new rigging.
  • Sails: condition of the cloth and stitching; tired sails show when you sail.
  • Keel and hull: the keel-to-hull joint and any knocks; on GRP hulls, watch for osmosis.
  • Auxiliary engine: hours, servicing and condition of the shaft or saildrive.
  • Deck gear: winches, furlers and autopilot.

An independent survey is even more advisable on a sailboat than on a motorboat.

Which licence you need

To skipper a sailboat over 6 metres you need at least the PNB (up to 8 metres) or the PER (up to 15 metres), in both cases with the sailing practice. On each listing I always give the licence as "or higher".

What it costs to keep

A sailboat uses little fuel, but rigging and sails are big expenses every so often, and the berth for a 10 to 12 metre sailboat adds up. Run the numbers with the yearly cost calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How often should sailboat rigging be replaced?

As a guide, it is usually inspected thoroughly every few years and replaced from around 10 to 15 years, depending on use and condition. With no invoices, budget for it.

What licence do I need for a sailboat in Spain?

The PNB (up to 8 metres) or the PER (up to 15 metres), in both cases with the sailing practice.

Is a sailboat cheaper to keep than a motorboat?

On fuel, yes. But rigging, sails and berth (sailboats tend to be longer) even things out quite a bit.
