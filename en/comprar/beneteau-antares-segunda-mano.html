Used Beneteau Antares for sale

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

The Antares is Beneteau's range for fishing, day trips or sleeping aboard, with an enclosed wheelhouse that keeps the wind and sun off. It is one of the boats I am asked for most, and one of the most searched-for on the used market.

About the range

There are two very different eras. Earlier-generation Antares almost always have an inboard diesel and a sea-going hull, and they are still in high demand. The current ones (Antares 7, 8, 9, 11 and 12) run outboards and offer more living space and more deck. When comparing listings, always compare within the same generation.

What to check on a used Beneteau Antares

  • Engine: hours, service invoices and, on outboards, the year and whether it was under warranty.
  • Wheelhouse: window seals and sliding doors; leaks are common if they have not been looked after.
  • Electronics: many have a sounder, plotter and autopilot; check they work and how old they are.
  • Hull and underwater: antifouling, anodes and gelcoat, especially if it has always been afloat.

Guide prices

For the Antares 8 (outboard) I have published ranges: 2016 to 2018, €45,000 to €73,000; 2019 to 2021, €67,000 to €100,000; 2022 onwards, €80,000 to €110,000. Wide spread depending on engine and equipment. Model guides with more detail: Antares 8.

Which licence you need

Up to 8 metres, a PNB or higher is enough; Antares over 8 metres need a PER or higher.

Frequently asked questions

Which Antares suits me?

It depends on use: for fishing and day trips with two or three people, an Antares 7 or 8 is plenty; to sleep aboard with family, look from the 9 upwards. Tell me your use and budget and I will tell you which fit.

Outboard or inboard?

An outboard is easier and cheaper to maintain and leaves the cockpit clear; an inboard diesel uses less fuel on long passages. I explain it in the propulsion guide.
