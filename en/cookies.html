Cookie policy

Cookies and storage we use

We store your choice in pm_privacy_v2, in your browser's local storage, for 12 months. It includes the decision, its date and the version of this notice. It is necessary to respect your choice and is not used for advertising.

NameProvider and purposeDuration
_ga and _ga_*Google Analytics: browsing statistics, only with your consent.Up to 12 months from creation.

We don't activate Google Ads, Google Signals or the Meta pixel. If we add advertising purposes in the future, we will update this information and ask for the corresponding consent before activating them. Rejecting analytics doesn't stop you from using the forms or contacting us.

On guide and tool pages, pm_news remembers that you closed the news notice for 30 days, or that you already signed up. This preference doesn't store your email. It is automatically deleted after 12 months at the latest.

Change or withdraw your consent

The controls are also available in the footer. Withdrawing consent disables measurement and deletes the analytics cookies accessible to this site. If the tool was already loaded, the page will reload to stop it. You can also delete all site data from your browser.

Data and transfers

Google Analytics processes browsing information and pseudonymous identifiers. This should not be confused with anonymous information. See Google's transfer safeguards and our privacy policy, which identifies the data controller and explains your rights.
