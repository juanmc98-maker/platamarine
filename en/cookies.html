Cookie policy

Cookies and storage we use

We store your choice in pm_privacy_v3, in your browser's local storage, for 12 months. It includes the decision, its date and the version of this notice. It is needed to respect your choice and is not used for advertising.

NameProvider and purposeDuration
_ga and _ga_*Google Analytics: browsing statistics, only with your consent.Up to 12 months from creation.
_gcl_*Google Ads: to know whether one of our ads led to a visit or an enquiry. Only if you accept it (“Accept all” or “Settings”).Up to 90 days.
_fbp and _fbcMeta (Facebook/Instagram): to measure how well our ads work. Only if you accept it (“Accept all” or “Settings”).Up to 90 days.

Under “Settings” you can turn analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Google Ads and Meta) on separately; “Accept all” turns both on and “Reject all” turns both off. We do not activate Google Signals or use your data for personalised advertising beyond that measurement. With your analytics consent, we also store in pm_attr (local storage, not a cookie) the source channel of your visit —for example utm_source, utm_campaign or an ad click identifier (gclid) if you arrived from an ad, or the website that linked to us— for a maximum of 90 days, so that we know which channel generated each enquiry. It is text taken from the web address itself or from the page that brought you here; it is not shared with third parties or used to show you advertising, and it is deleted if you withdraw your analytics consent. If we add new purposes, we will update this information and ask for your consent before activating them. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the forms or getting in touch.

On guide and tool pages, pm_news remembers for 30 days that you have closed the updates notice, or that you have already signed up. This preference does not store your email address. It is deleted automatically after 12 months at most.

Change or withdraw your consent

The controls are also available in the footer. Withdrawing consent turns off measurement and deletes the analytics cookies accessible to this website. If the tool was already loaded, the page reloads to stop it. You can also delete all site data from your browser.

Data and transfers

Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta process browsing information and pseudonymous identifiers. This should not be confused with anonymous information. See Google's transfer safeguards, Meta's and our privacy policy, which identifies the data controller and explains your rights.
