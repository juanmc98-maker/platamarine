What matters most

Stable when the sea gets rough

It is the point the yard stresses most. According to SKIPPER, the AeroQuad™ 4-step hull and the 24° deep V are designed to keep the 38 NC+ controlled and planted at high speed, up to 70 knots, and to cut through the waves instead of slamming into them: far less pounding than a conventional hull and a drier deck, because the water is pushed outwards.

  1. 24° deep VDesigned to cut through the waves instead of jumping over them, without the hard slam of a conventional hull.
  2. AeroQuad™ 4-step hullFeeds air under the hull: less friction, earlier planing and lower consumption.
  3. Dry deckSteps and spray rails push the water outwards, not upwards.

A closer look at the 38 NC+

The 38 NC+ updates one of the most seaworthy boats in its class. It now measures 12.00 m and gets a carbon hardtop with an integrated glass windscreen, wiper and washer, side deflectors, a longer swim platform and new pilot and co-pilot seats.

The yard designs it for three roles: fast day boat, superyacht tender or chase boat, and a platform to enjoy at anchor, with large sunbeds, a full wet bar and an optional head.

What sets it apart

Glass windscreenIntegrated in the carbon hardtop, with wiper, washer and side deflectors.

Frequently asked questions

What licence do I need?

All three are 12.40 m or shorter, so in Spain the PER (Recreational Boat Skipper) or a higher licence is enough. See licences.

Does the price include taxes and transport?

No. Prices are ex-works and exclude VAT. Transport, registration and, if registered in Spain, the special registration tax (IEDMT), which depends on use and flag, are extra. I detail it in the offer.

Who do I pay?

Everything is paid directly to SKIPPER (BSK Marine). The deposit never goes through me.

How long does the build take?

It depends on the model, the extras and the yard’s workload. SKIPPER confirms the lead time in writing in the official offer.
