Boating in Menorca

Why a high-performance RIB makes sense in Menorca

Menorca is all about the wind. When the tramontana blows, the north coast gets difficult and the southern coves become the refuge; when it drops, the north coast is among the most beautiful in the Balearics. With a fast boat you can pick the good side of the island each day and change plans without losing the day.

According to SKIPPER, all three models share the AeroQuad™ 4-step hull and deep 24° V, designed to stay stable at high speed and cut the waves rather than slam into them. On an island where the northerly often leaves a sea running, that behaviour is what you notice most in terms of comfort.

And Mallorca is close. Crossing the Menorca Channel from Ciutadella to spend the day on the other island is a short passage, but in open water; with a boat that has the range and a hull designed for these conditions, you do it with more peace of mind.

Good to know

MahónOne of the largest natural harbours in the Mediterranean, long and sheltered.
TramontanaThe north of the island is exposed to the northerly; the south usually offers more shelter.
Ciutadella and FornellsGood starting points for the west and for the north coast.
Menorca ChannelBetween Ciutadella and Mallorca there is a short crossing, but in open water.

Frequently asked questions

Which model handles the tramontana best?

In a strong tramontana the sensible thing is not to go out, or to seek the shelter of the south coast. For days with moderate wind, the 38 NC+, with its closed glass windscreen, and the 42 NC, the largest in the range, are the most comfortable; all three are design category B.

Can I cross to Mallorca and back in a day?

Yes, the Menorca Channel is a short passage. Even so, it is open water: check the forecast and your fuel before you leave.

Where can I anchor?

In the Balearics it is forbidden to anchor on posidonia, and in summer some coves have mooring buoys or regulated anchoring. Check the permitted areas before dropping the anchor.

Can I try a SKIPPER before ordering?

There is no demo boat in Spain, but the yard can arrange a sea trial, including a factory visit. If you like, I come with you.
