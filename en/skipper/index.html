SKIPPER ACE 360

The new one. Launched in May 2026.

From €329,000 +VATAsk about the ACE 360
26 photos

The ACE 360 is the evolution of the 34 NC, the model that made SKIPPER’s name. It has been redesigned from top to bottom for owners who want speed with full control, as a day boat or as a tender to a larger yacht.

It combines the AeroQuad™ 4-step hull with the InfinityDeck™ layout: the beam stays almost constant from stern to bow, so there is more usable space and wide walkways to move around while under way.

What sets it apart

Rated for 14The yard certifies it for 14 people. In Spain, as a recreational boat, the limit is 12 passengers.
Planes at ~11 knotsAccording to the yard, the steps get it on the plane sooner and with less power.
2,155 litres of storageIntegrated storage spread throughout the boat.
Modular protectionTransparent wind and spray protection you fit depending on the day.

AeroQuad™ · InfinityDeck™

Why they run the way they do

What the yard has built into the hull and deck of all three models.

  1. AeroQuad™ 4-step hullSKIPPER’s patent, developed with scale models, full-size prototypes and fluid simulation. The steps feed air under the hull: less friction, earlier planing and lower consumption.
  2. 24° deep VCuts through the waves instead of jumping over them. Steps and spray rails push water outwards, not upwards: a dry deck.
  3. InfinityDeck™ layoutThe beam is carried from the aft U-shaped lounge to the bow sunbed: more usable area and wide, safe walkways.
  4. Composite constructionA rigid structure designed for open water. Carbon-epoxy option to save weight.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can I take?

In Spain, a recreational boat may carry no more than 12 passengers (Royal Decree 1434/1999); above that it is treated as a passenger vessel. So although the yard certifies the ACE 360 for 14 people, registered here as a recreational boat the limit is 12 passengers. The exact figure is set by its papers: check with the Harbour Master.

What licence do I need?

All three are 12.40 m or shorter, so in Spain the PER (Recreational Boat Skipper) or a higher licence is enough. See licences.

Does the price include taxes and transport?

No. Prices are ex-works and exclude VAT. Transport, registration and, if registered in Spain, the special registration tax (IEDMT), which depends on use and flag, are extra. I detail it in the offer.

Who do I pay?

Everything is paid directly to SKIPPER (BSK Marine). The deposit never goes through me.

How long does the build take?

It depends on the model, the extras and the yard’s workload. SKIPPER confirms the lead time in writing in the official offer.

Can it be used as a yacht tender?

Yes. The yard designs the 38 NC+ and 42 NC also as superyacht tenders and chase boats, and the ACE 360 as a day boat or tender.

Where is it built?

In Koropi, on the outskirts of Athens (Greece), at the BSK Marine yard, founded in 2015.

Who is behind it

SKIPPER is the brand of BSK Marine, a yard founded in 2015 in Koropi, near Athens. In a few years its RIBs have spread across the Mediterranean.
