AeroQuad™ · InfinityDeck™

Why they run like this

What the shipyard has built into the hull and deck of all three models.

  1. AeroQuad™ 4-step hullSKIPPER's patent: the steps feed air under the hull, cut drag and get the boat on the plane sooner and more efficiently.
  2. 24° deep VCuts through the waves instead of slamming on them. Drier and more comfortable when the sea gets serious.
  3. InfinityDeck™ layoutThe beam stays constant from stern to bow: wide side decks and more usable space for the same length.
  4. Composite constructionA rigid structure designed for open water, with big-boat finishes.

Shipyard's description, from its technical sheets.

What you won't pay extra for

Standard equipment shared by all three models:

  • Hardtop
  • Bow thruster
  • Remote-controlled anchor
  • Raymarine screen
  • VHF and charts
  • JL Audio sound
  • Wet bar with fridge
  • Fresh water washdown
  • LED deck and underwater lights
  • Battery charger and shore power

On the 38 NC+ and 42 NC you also get a carbon hardtop and real carbon dashboard (depending on configuration).

How to buy a SKIPPER

  1. We talkYou tell me how you'll use the boat and we choose model, engines and colours.
  2. Official quoteSKIPPER issues the quote for your exact configuration.
  3. Contract with the shipyardYou sign with SKIPPER and the deposit is paid directly to the shipyard, never to me.
  4. Build and deliveryI follow the build with you and coordinate transport, registration and commissioning.

Who's behind it

SKIPPER is the brand of BSK Marine, a shipyard founded in 2015 in Koropi, just outside Athens. In a few years its RIBs have spread across the Mediterranean.
