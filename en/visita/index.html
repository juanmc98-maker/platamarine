You can also write to juan@platamarine.com. If no confirmation appears, please check before sending again.

How it works

  1. You choose the boat, the day and the time. Sundays are on request: tell me the time of day and I will suggest a time.
  2. I arrange it with the owner. Until then, the viewing stays pending.
  3. I message you on WhatsApp to confirm the day, time and meeting point. I give you the exact marina at that point, to protect the owner’s privacy.

Before you come

If it is your first boat or you are deciding between several, the guide on what to check when buying second-hand will help. And if something comes up, just tell me on WhatsApp and we will move the viewing.
