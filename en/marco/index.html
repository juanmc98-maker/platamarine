How buying works

  1. You tell me which model, engines and options you want.
  2. I send you a written quote: boat, options, engines, transport and taxes.
  3. The order is placed with the deposit set in the contract and the builder builds the boat. Delivery time is confirmed in writing for each order.
  4. Transport, registration and handover in Catalonia.

How I see it

These are RIBs for people who want a large, stable boat that can be customised almost completely. The E-Motion cabin models let you sleep on board; the open models give more deck space for the day. I help you choose the model and engines for the way you use the boat and the berth you have.

Shall I prepare a quote?

Tell me which model, engines and options you are interested in and I will send you the price in writing, with no obligation.
