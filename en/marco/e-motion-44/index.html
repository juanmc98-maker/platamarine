Frequently asked questions

Which licence do I need?

Spanish PER or higher, given its 13.50 m length. This is a guide only: please check with your sailing school or the harbour authority.

What does the price include?

The boat with its standard equipment, ex works (Italy) and excluding taxes. Engines, transport to Barcelona, VAT, registration tax and registration are extra and detailed in the quote.

Can I choose colours and finishes?

Yes. Every Mar.Co is built to order: besides the options in the price list, the shipyard does bespoke finishes. Tell me what you have in mind and I will ask.

What if I already have engines?

It can be looked at. What matters is that the power and shaft length suit the boat: we check it with the shipyard before ordering.
