    Is it for you?

    Who the TwentyThree is for

    Who it suits

    The smallest and lightest Mar.Co: 7.40 m and 930 kg. It suits anyone who wants an open RIB for day trips with family or friends without a big boat to look after. It is the only model in the range with CE design category C and the only one that can be skippered with a Spanish PNB licence (a guide only: please check with your school or the harbour authority).

    Compared with the TwentySix

    The TwentySix grows to 8.43 m with a 3.25 m beam, is CE-rated for 16 people instead of 14, adds a toilet in the console and a sunbed at the stern as well, and is category B. The TwentyThree keeps to a 2.87 m beam, with a fibreglass console, a 70 l insulated icebox, a dinette table and a bow sunbed. Less boat, but easier to move, store and maintain.

    Engines

    It takes a single outboard, with a maximum rated power of 224 kW (≈ 300 hp) and a 300 l stainless steel fuel tank. Engines are not in the shipyard price list: you can go with Mercury, which is what Mar.Co fits, or with another brand. I quote them separately depending on how you will use the boat.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which licence do I need?

    Spanish PNB or higher, given its 7.40 m length. This is a guide only: please check with your sailing school or the harbour authority.

    What does the price include?

    The boat with its standard equipment, ex works (Italy) and excluding taxes. Engines, transport to Barcelona, VAT, registration tax and registration are extra and detailed in the quote.

    Can I choose colours and finishes?

    Yes. Every Mar.Co is built to order: besides the options in the price list, the shipyard does bespoke finishes. Tell me what you have in mind and I will ask.

    What if I already have engines?

    It can be looked at. What matters is that the power and shaft length suit the boat: we check it with the shipyard before ordering.
