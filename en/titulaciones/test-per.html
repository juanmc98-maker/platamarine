Licences · Practice test

PER practice test

Original questions by Plata MarineUpdated September 2026

Multiple-choice questions like the ones in the exam, with four options and an explanation for each answer. Choose a mode, answer, then mark it. Marking applies the same limits as the real exam: 13 errors in total, plus the fail-limit sections on buoyage, COLREGs (RIPA) and chart work.

The questions are original to Plata Marine, written from the official PER syllabus (RD 875/2014 and the Resolution of 19 December 2025). They are practice material: not official exam questions, and don't replace them. If you spot an error, let me know and I'll fix it.
