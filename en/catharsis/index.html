Catharsis 930: a 9.30-metre outboard cruiser

A boat with a wheelhouse, two cabins and a bathroom, made for day trips or a few days on board with the family. Built by Avila Marin in Izmir (Turkey) and ordered from the factory: I help you choose engines and equipment. I am their agent in Catalonia.

  • Built in Turkey by Avila Marin
  • Vacuum-infused hull
  • Two cabins, bathroom with shower and galley
  • For twin outboard engines: not included, fitted to your choice and quoted separately
  • Delivery: about 90 days from order, plus transport
  • Transport from Turkey to Barcelona: not included, quoted separately

Agent in Catalonia

The boat

Catharsis 930

Wheelhouse with a three-panel sliding aluminium door, cockpit with telescopic table and shower, forward cabin with double bed and a guest cabin. Standard equipment includes electric windlass, fridge, two-burner hob, electric toilet, LED searchlight, service batteries and stainless steel tanks.
