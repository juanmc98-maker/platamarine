A boat with a wheelhouse, two cabins and a bathroom, made for day trips or a few days on board with the family. Built by Avila Marin and ordered from the factory: I help you choose engines and equipment. I am their agent in Catalonia. Builder's photos: a demo unit, some taken at a boat show. They may show optional equipment.

Deck

A look from above

Tap the numbers to see each area.

  1. 1 · Stern platform. Swim platform on both sides of the engines, with a telescopic 4-step stainless steel ladder.
  2. 2 · Cockpit. Sofa, telescopic table and shower. The photos show the optional summer awning.
  3. 3 · Wheelhouse. Enclosed, with a three-panel sliding aluminium door. The solar panels on the roof are optional.
  4. 4 · Side decks. To walk forward on both sides, with 316 stainless guardrails and handrails.
  5. 5 · Bow sunbed. A place to lie in the sun ahead of the wheelhouse, with the electric windlass at the bow.
  6. 6 · Engines. The boat in the photos has twin outboards. Engines are not included: you choose them and they are quoted separately.

Cockpit and bow

Good to be outside

Cockpit with table

U-shaped sofa with a telescopic chrome-leg table, cockpit shower and LED step lights. The sliding door into the wheelhouse opens fully.

Bow and side decks

Bow sunbed and a 900 W electric windlass with foot switches and a 316 stainless bow roller. You get there along the side decks, with handrails along the wheelhouse.

Wheelhouse

An enclosed saloon with a view

Saloon and galley

Telescopic table, sofa and a galley with two-burner hob, sink and 65-litre fridge. Marine laminate floor, LED ceiling and floor lights, and USB, 12 V and 220 V sockets.

Helm

Dual-position folding helm seat, windscreen wiper with washer and a remote-controlled LED searchlight. A 9" or 12" Raymarine plotter is optional.

Interior

To sleep on board

Master cabin

Double bed with storage underneath, wardrobe, 50 × 50 cm hatch and two portholes. There is also a guest cabin with bed, wardrobe and porthole. It sleeps 4 in total (2 in each cabin), and the dinette table converts into a bed.

Bathroom

Large-bowl electric toilet, washbasin, mixer tap with shower head and porthole. Wood panels on walls and doors.

Engines

You choose the engines

Engines

You can order the 930 without engines or with twin 250 hp outboards (Mercury or Suzuki) fitted for €49,490 + taxes; configure it below. If you prefer another brand or power, I will send you a written quote. Maximum rated power is 447 kW (about 600 hp). The boat in the photos has twin Mercury outboards. The builder does not publish speed or fuel figures, as they depend heavily on the engines fitted. If you are interested in a specific set-up, I will ask for them in writing.

How buying works

  1. You tell me which engines and options you want.
  2. I send you a written quote: boat, options, engines, transport and taxes.
  3. The order is placed with the deposit set in the contract and the builder builds the boat.
  4. Transport, registration and handover in Catalonia.

How I see it

I see it for a couple or a family who want days in coves and the odd night on board, with an enclosed wheelhouse to stretch the season. With outboards the engines sit outside the boat and maintenance is usually simpler. Under Spanish rules it needs the PER licence or higher.
