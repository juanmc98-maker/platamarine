How buying works

  1. You tell me which model, engines and options you want.
  2. I send you a written quote: boat, options, engines, transport and taxes.
  3. The order is placed with the deposit set in the contract and the builder builds the boat.
  4. Transport, registration and handover.

How I see it

I like the project: a 9-metre catamaran has the cockpit of a much bigger motorboat, runs flat and moves less at anchor, which you appreciate in the Balearic coves. With outboards, maintenance is usually simpler. If you are torn between the Open and the Cruiser, the question is whether you will sleep on board.
