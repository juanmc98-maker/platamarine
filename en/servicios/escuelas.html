Boating schools: where to get your licence

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

To skipper a boat in Spain you need the licence that matches it. Here I’m gathering boating schools by area so you know where to get the Licencia de Navegación, PNB, PER or Patrón de Yate. Which licence you need for each boat is explained on the licences page.

Nobody pays to be listed here. I only include schools that have written to me and that I’ve spoken to, and the details are the ones each school gives me; if anything changes, write to me and I’ll fix it. If there is ever a paid collaboration with a school, it will be shown on its listing.

Schools by area

I’m just starting this list and there are no schools published yet. If you’re looking for one now, write to me with your area and the licence you want and I’ll tell you what I know.

Ask me on WhatsApp

How to choose a school

  • What the price includes. Book or online test platform, practical sessions, radio course, and whether you can repeat classes or practicals if you fail. Exam fees and the card itself are almost never included.
  • Where the practicals take place. Which port and which boat. Practical hours drag on if the port is far from home.
  • Timetables. Weekdays, weekends or online theory, and which exam session they prepare you for.
  • Sailing. If you want to skipper sailing boats with the PER, you also need the sailing practicals. Ask whether the school offers them.
  • Extension and passages. If you plan to go to the Balearics or skipper larger boats, ask about the PER extension from the start; some schools do it on a passage.

Do you run a boating school?

If you’d like to be listed here, write to me with the school’s name, where you teach theory and practicals, which licences you prepare for and your website. Being listed is free; I just ask that you treat well the people who come from here.
