Boat inspection, paperwork, haul-out, transport and insurance: I put you in touch with who I'd use

CataloniaReply within 24 working hours

Buying or selling a boat is only part of it. After that come the technical inspection, the name change, the yearly haul-out and the insurance, and the hard part isn't doing them, it's knowing who to call. Tell me what you need and I'll tell you who I'd use. It costs you nothing.

To be clear: I recommend companies I've worked with or have a partnership with, and for some of those recommendations I may receive compensation from the company. I never charge you for putting you in touch, and whatever price they quote you is theirs, with no markup added. If a company stops deserving the recommendation, I drop it. And if you'd rather I just gave you the contact without passing on your details, tell me in the form's checkbox. If you'd rather call yourself, there's the directory of vetted companies.

Boat technical inspection (ITB)

If your boat has to pass the technical inspection (or you're not sure whether it does, which is the most common case), tell me where it is, its length and when it expires, and I'll put you in touch with an approved inspection body working in your area. Before that, you can read the inspection guide: who needs it, how often, and what they check.

Nautical paperwork agent: name change, registration, import

Changing ownership of a used boat, registering a new one, or flagging an imported one can be done on your own, but a nautical paperwork agency that handles this every week saves you trips to the Harbour Master's office and mistakes that later cost months. I explain it in the guide on paperwork and name changes. If you'd rather someone else handled it, tell me about your case.

Boat transport: by road or under way

Boat insurance

Third-party liability insurance is mandatory for any motorboat, and for a boat above a certain value it's also worth taking out hull and machinery cover. I'm not an insurance broker and I don't take part in arranging the policy; what I can do is tell you which nautical insurance broker I work with and what cover I'd look at. Message me on WhatsApp with the length, year and approximate value of the boat and I'll reply the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge me anything for putting me in touch?

No. If the company I recommend compensates me for it, they pay it out of their own margin, and the price they give you is the same as they'd give any other client.

What happens with my data?

I keep it to reply to you. Only if you tick the corresponding box do I pass it to the company I recommend, so it can contact you. If you don't tick it, I give you the contact myself and you decide whether to call. You can ask me to delete it whenever you want.

What if I'm not in Catalonia?

My contacts are mostly on the Catalan coast, with some in the Balearics. If your boat is elsewhere, I'll tell you plainly rather than recommend blindly.
