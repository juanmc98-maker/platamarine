Services · Boat paperwork

Change of ownership and flag for your boat

By Juan MoranteUpdated November 2026

If you are buying a boat in Spain, or you have already agreed the deal with the seller, I handle the paperwork so it ends up in your name: the change of ownership and, if needed, the change of flag. A nautical agency I work with processes it, and you only deal with me.

What I handle

  • Change of ownership. Transferring the boat from seller to buyer, between private individuals or when buying from a company.
  • Change of flag. Bringing a boat from another country under the Spanish flag, or flagging it abroad if that suits you better.
  • Change of list. If you are moving the boat from private use (7th list) to charter (6th list), or the other way round.
  • Check before you pay. Before paying, we check the boat is registered to the person selling it and that the paperwork is in order.

How it works

  1. You tell me about your case. Which boat, where it is, which flag it has and whether it is already bought or agreed.
  2. I tell you what is needed. The list of documents for your case and a fixed written quote before we start.
  3. We process it. The agency handles the procedure and I keep you updated until the papers are in your name.

Documents usually required

Every case is different, but this is what is usually required for a change of ownership between private individuals:

  • ID card, NIE or passport of buyer and seller.
  • Purchase contract signed by both.
  • The boat’s documents: registration or seaworthiness certificate and, if it has one, the registry entry sheet.
  • Proof that the purchase tax has been paid (between private individuals, usually the regional transfer tax, ITP).
  • If the flag changes, the papers from the original registry and its deletion.

I will confirm the exact list for your case before we start.

If you have not bought it yet

Even better: this is the time to avoid surprises. Before paying, it is worth checking that the seller is the registered owner, that the boat has no charges against it and that the papers match the boat (length, engine, hull number). If you want to reserve it, I recommend doing so with a deposit agreement (arras).

FAQ

How much does it cost?

It depends on the boat and the procedure. Before we start I send you a fixed written quote, with fees and tax shown separately so you can see what each item is.

Do I need to go anywhere in person?

It is usually done with the paperwork. If your case needs a signature in person, I will tell you from the start.

Can I use this if I did not buy the boat through you?

Yes. It does not matter where you found it: if the deal is agreed or the boat is already yours, I will help you with the papers.

Who pays the purchase tax?

In a sale between private individuals it is usually the buyer. You can see the details by region in the ITP guide.

For guidance only: requirements and taxes can change and depend on each case. We confirm everything in writing before we start.
