Who to call in Catalonia: inspection, paperwork, boatyard and flooring

By Juan MoranteReviewed in September 2026

The question I get most after the price is "and who do I call?". Here I keep the companies I've vetted, by area, so you don't have to search. It's short on purpose: I'd rather have a few reliable names than many filler ones. If you'd rather I looked into it for you, use the service forms.

Nobody pays to appear here. I show the direct phone number for official inspection bodies and for companies I have a partnership with (if I put you in touch with them and you hire them, they may compensate me; it costs you nothing extra). For the rest, I give you the contact myself, with my opinion, through the form. The details are each company's public information; if anything is wrong or missing, write to me and I'll fix it.

Inspection bodies (ITB)

The only ones authorised to issue the seaworthiness certificate. Official list from the Ministry of Transport, July 2026. Choose the one with a centre closest to the boat.
