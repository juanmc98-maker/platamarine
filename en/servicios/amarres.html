Moorings for transfer or rent

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 2026

Many boats sell better with a berth, and many people looking for a boat have nowhere to keep it. Here I will list berths from clients who want to transfer or rent them, always with their permission and using the details they give me.

Available moorings

Right now I have no moorings listed. If you are looking for one, tell me the area, your boat's length and beam, and whether you want to rent or take over a berth, and I'll tell you what I know.

Ask about a berth on WhatsApp

What you should know

  • “Buying” a berth is rarely buying the land. In most marinas what is transferred is a right of use until the marina's concession ends. Always check until which year it runs.
  • Transfers usually need approval. Many marinas and clubs must authorise the change of holder and may charge a fee; some clubs require membership.
  • Ask for the numbers first. Annual maintenance fee, water and electricity, and any pending levies.
  • Check the dimensions. Maximum length and beam of the berth, and whether your boat fits comfortably.

Do you have a berth to transfer or rent out?

Write to me with the marina, the dimensions, whether it is owned (right of use) or rented, when the concession ends and the price you have in mind. If it fits, I will list it here with no upfront cost; we agree the terms first, in writing.

Guidance only: every marina and concession has its own rules. Before buying or renting a berth, confirm with the marina or club and, if needed, with a lawyer or agency.
