Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Data controller: Juan Morante Cruz, NIF 77123920K, C/ Tetuán 92, 08203 Sabadell (Barcelona), juan@platamarine.com.

What data is processed and why

The data you send me by WhatsApp, by email or through the website form (name, phone number, details of your boat or of the one you are looking for) is used solely to value and manage the sale of your boat or the search for the one you want to buy, and to answer your enquiries.

The main form for selling your boat and the alerts, tools, updates and services forms send the data to Google Apps Script so that the request can be handled. If, instead of submitting the form, you prefer to write to me by WhatsApp or email, the website only prepares the message for you and you send it yourself from your own app.

Results by email, alerts, updates and service requests

With some of the website's tools (for example, the PER practice test) you can ask me to send you the result by email. This is optional: the result is shown on screen anyway without you giving any data. If you ask for it, I keep your email address, the date, the result and the record of your consent in order to send it to you, and I delete that data after one year.

Only if you expressly tick the updates box (it is not ticked by default) will I use that address to send you, from time to time, articles, guides and the occasional commercial offer or news from Plata Marine (boats I'm handling, promotions). You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any of those emails with "unsubscribe" or by writing to juan@platamarine.com, and I will delete your address. I do not sell or pass on addresses to anyone, nor do I use them for anything else.

This data is stored in a Google spreadsheet and the emails are sent through Google. If in future I use a newsletter platform, it will also act as a processor and I will state this here.

Boat alerts. If you create an alert (on the alerts page or by ticking the box when taking the "which boat do I need" test), I keep your name, your phone number, your email address if you give it, and what you are looking for (type, length, budget and area) so that I can write to you when a boat that matches comes up for sale with me. The alert is kept for twelve months; after that I ask you once whether you are still looking and, if you do not reply, I delete it. You can ask me to delete it earlier by replying to any email.

Updates notice. On the guides and articles pages a small notice may appear inviting you to sign up for updates. Your email address is only stored if you enter it and tick the box; if you close the notice, it will not appear again for a month and no personal data is stored (only a marker in your browser so that it is not shown again).

Requests for ITB (boat inspection), gestoría (paperwork agency), boatyard or insurance. On the services page you can ask me to put you in touch with a company. I keep the form data in order to reply to you and tell you who I would use. Your data is only passed on to that company if, after I tell you who they are, you give me your express permission; otherwise I give you the contact details and you decide. For some of these recommendations I may receive compensation from the company, which I state on the page itself; I do not charge you anything for it.

Legal basis and retention

The legal basis is the performance of the pre-contractual or contractual relationship you request, including enquiries about our services. For alerts, results by email and updates, the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting processing carried out before then. Data relating to a sale or purchase is kept for as long as the relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the applicable statutory periods; data for results by email and service requests, one year; alert data, twelve months or until you ask me to delete the alert; updates data, until you unsubscribe.

Recipients

Data is not disclosed to third parties except where required by law, where necessary for the transaction (for example, a gestoría for the change of ownership, a surveyor or an insurer), always with your knowledge, or where, in a service request, you give me express permission for the recommended company to contact you after you know who they are. I do not pass your data on to other companies so that they can offer you their products or services. The messaging and email services you use have their own policies.

Viewing requests. If you ask to see a boat from the viewings page, I keep your name, phone number, email, the boat, the day and time and, if you provide them, your licence and notes, in order to arrange the viewing. The request is recorded in my viewings sheet and in my Google calendar, and I receive an email notice. To arrange it I tell the owner, or the intermediary holding the sale mandate, your name and the day; nothing else. Legal basis: your own request, as a step prior to a possible purchase. I keep this data for up to twelve months after the viewing, unless you ask me to delete it earlier.

Delivery quotes. If you ask me from a boat page for a quote to move it by road or by sea, I keep your name, phone number, email and the destination in order to request the quote and send it to you. I only give the transport company or skipper the boat and the destination; not your details, unless you later allow me to put you in touch. Legal basis: your own request. I keep this data for up to twelve months, unless you ask me to delete it earlier.

Boats offered together with other intermediaries. Some boats are marketed in collaboration with another broker or nautical agency that holds the owner’s sale mandate. If you are interested in one of them, I will tell you so first and, if you wish to go ahead, I will pass on your name and the details needed to arrange the viewing and handle the transaction. That intermediary will use them only for that transaction, as an independent controller, and for no other purpose.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection by writing to juan@platamarine.com. If you consider that the processing is not correct, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

Providers and international transfers

We use GitHub to host the website; Google Apps Script, Sheets and Calendar to receive and organise enquiries and viewings; and Dinahosting email (juan@platamarine.com) to reply to them. If you write to me or leave your phone number, I may reply via WhatsApp Business (a WhatsApp/Meta service). If you get in touch through a form in a Plata Marine ad on Facebook or Instagram, Meta collects that data and passes it to me; Meta also processes it under its own privacy policy. I do not send commercial emails to anyone who has not requested or authorised them. These services may process technical and contact data outside the European Economic Area. We do not send your name, phone number, email address or the content of your forms to Google Analytics or Meta: if you accept it, we only measure that a contact click has been made or that a form has been received.

Google describes its use of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for the entities and processing covered, and of standard contractual clauses, in its information on transfers. GitHub explains its safeguards and how it processes visitor data in its privacy statement. You can ask the data controller for information about, and a copy of, the safeguards applicable to your processing.

Analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity only if you accept analytics, and the Meta pixel only if you also accept marketing cookies, in the notice shown when you first visit. More information in the cookie policy.
