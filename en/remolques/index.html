Frequently asked questions

Does the trailer need registering?

Yes. In Spain, trailers over 750 kg GVW have their own number plate and pass the ITV inspection. All the trailers on this page are above that weight, so they come with a Spanish COC and technical sheet.

Which driving licence do I need?

With a B licence you can tow a trailer over 750 kg if the GVW of car and trailer together does not exceed 3,500 kg. Above that you need B96 (up to 4,250 kg) or BE.

How long does it take?

Lead time depends on the factory; I confirm it in writing when placing the order.

Do you have trailers for jet skis or bigger boats?

Yes, Goliath builds trailers for jet skis and for boats up to 3,500 kg. Tell me what you need and I will send you options.
