How to photograph your boat

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

Photos are the first thing a buyer sees. With your phone and about 20 minutes you can do a great job. Choose your type of boat and see the photos I need.

What boat do you have?

The 6 photos I need · RIB

  • 1Full side view at 3/4
  • 2Stern with the engine
  • 3From stern to bow
  • 4Console close-up
  • 5Engine from the side
  • 6On the trailer (if any)

Before taking the photos

Clean boatInside and out.
No coversShow the boat as it really is.
No personal itemsBuckets, bags, towels, loose lines.
Sunny dayWith the sun behind you, never facing it.

How to take them

Phone in landscapePortrait photos don’t work for the listing.
No zoom, no filtersTo frame, walk closer or further away.
Whole boatDon’t cut off the bow or the stern.
