Sell a boat · Costa Brava

Boat broker on the Costa Brava · selling your boat

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

If your boat is in Blanes, Sant Feliu de Guíxols, Palamós, L'Estartit, L'Escala, Roses, Empuriabrava or Llançà and you want to sell it without it eating your time, here is how I work.

Selling a boat on the Costa Brava

Here boats are used mostly for cove-hopping, and buyers look above all for open, sundeck and walkaround boats of 6 to 9 metres, RIBs and mid-sized cabin cruisers for the odd night on board. Many buyers come from outside the area, including France, and need to see the boat well explained before they travel.

What I do: I study the price against comparable boats, prepare a listing with good photos and a text that explains the boat, place it on my website and on the portals that work, screen enquiries and stay with you until signing and the change of ownership. If the boat comes with a transferable berth, I make the most of it in the listing.

How I work

  • Fees in writing. I set them out in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.
  • No exclusivity, no upfront payments. You can keep your own listing while we work.
  • We start with your photos and paperwork. I attend viewings when there is a serious buyer, depending on the boat and the area.
  • You deal with me directly throughout, by WhatsApp or email.

What I need to get started

Make, model and year, engines and hours, where the boat is and what kind of berth it has, whether the seaworthiness certificate is valid, and some recent photos. With that I can give you a first price guide, with no commitment. If it suits you, we sign the mandate and I get to work on the listing.

For good photos there is the photo guide, and if you want an idea first, see what affects your boat’s price.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to list a boat on the Costa Brava?

The earlier in the season, the better: February to June is when most people are looking. Boats also sell out of season, but it usually takes longer. I do not promise timescales: it depends on the boat, the price and its condition.

Does the berth affect the price?

Yes. Berths on the Costa Brava are scarce in summer, and a boat sold with a transferable berth usually attracts more interest. If you have one, tell me and I will mention it in the listing.

How much do you charge to sell my boat?

I set out my fees in writing in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.

Do I have to give you exclusivity?

No. I work without exclusivity and you can keep your own listing in the meantime.

Other areas where I work: Barcelona and Maresme · Tarragona · Balearic Islands
