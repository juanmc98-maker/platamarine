Sell a boat · Tarragona

Boat broker in Tarragona and the Costa Daurada · selling your boat

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

If your boat is in Calafell, Coma-ruga, Torredembarra, Tarragona, Salou, Cambrils, L'Ametlla de Mar, L'Ampolla or La Ràpita and you want to sell it without it eating your time, here is how I work.

Selling a boat in Tarragona and on the Costa Daurada

It is a coast with many yacht-club harbours and berths that are more affordable than in Barcelona or on the Costa Brava, which attracts buyers from all over Catalonia, Aragon and inland Spain looking for a first boat or a bigger one. Motorboats and mid-sized cabin cruisers, cruising yachts and fishing boats sell well, especially further south and around the Ebro Delta.

What I do: I study the price against comparable boats, prepare a listing with good photos and a text that explains the boat, place it on my website and on the portals that work, screen enquiries and stay with you until signing and the change of ownership.

How I work

  • Fees in writing. I set them out in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.
  • No exclusivity, no upfront payments. You can keep your own listing while we work.
  • We start with your photos and paperwork. I attend viewings when there is a serious buyer, depending on the boat and the area.
  • You deal with me directly throughout, by WhatsApp or email.

What I need to get started

Make, model and year, engines and hours, where the boat is and what kind of berth it has, whether the seaworthiness certificate is valid, and some recent photos. With that I can give you a first price guide, with no commitment. If it suits you, we sign the mandate and I get to work on the listing.

For good photos there is the photo guide, and if you want an idea first, see what affects your boat’s price.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also work in the Terres de l'Ebre?

Yes, down to La Ràpita and Les Cases d'Alcanar. At first we work with your photos and paperwork, and I attend viewings when there is a serious buyer.

If my berth belongs to a yacht club, can the buyer keep it?

It depends on each club’s rules: in many, the berth is tied to membership and cannot simply be transferred. Before mentioning it in the listing we check with the club so as not to promise anything that cannot be delivered.

How much do you charge to sell my boat?

I set out my fees in writing in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.

Do I have to give you exclusivity?

No. I work without exclusivity and you can keep your own listing in the meantime.

Other areas where I work: Costa Brava · Barcelona and Maresme · Balearic Islands

My fees and the specific terms are set in writing in the sale mandate. Plata Marine acts as an intermediary: it does not buy or sell boats in its own name. The information on this page is for guidance only and is not binding.
