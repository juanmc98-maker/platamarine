Sell a boat · Balearic Islands

Boat broker in the Balearic Islands · selling your boat

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 2026

If your boat is in Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza or Formentera and you want to sell it, here is how I work. I am based in Catalonia and work in the Balearics when the boat justifies it, with the photos, paperwork and buyer lined up first.

Selling a boat in the Balearic Islands

The islands have plenty of quality boats and plenty of buyers from mainland Spain and abroad who consider buying there or bringing the boat back to Catalonia or Valencia. That is why the listing has to explain the boat and its condition very well, so that buyers only travel when they are genuinely interested.

What I do: I study the price against comparable boats on the islands and the mainland, prepare the listing, place it on my website and on the portals that work, screen enquiries and arrange the viewing and sea trial with you. If the buyer is from elsewhere, I explain the options for taking the boat away.

How I work

  • Fees in writing. I set them out in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.
  • No exclusivity, no upfront payments. You can keep your own listing while we work.
  • We start with your photos and paperwork. I attend viewings when there is a serious buyer, depending on the boat and the area.
  • You deal with me directly throughout, by WhatsApp or email.

What I need to get started

Make, model and year, engines and hours, where the boat is and what kind of berth it has, whether the seaworthiness certificate is valid, and some recent photos. With that I can give you a first price guide, with no commitment. If it suits you, we sign the mandate and I get to work on the listing.

For good photos there is the photo guide, and if you want an idea first, see what affects your boat’s price.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to come to the islands to sell my boat?

Not at first: we start with your photos and paperwork. I travel when there is a serious buyer and the boat justifies it, and we discuss it beforehand so there are no surprises.

What tax does the buyer of a used boat in the Balearics pay?

If buying from a private seller, the transfer tax (ITP) of the region where the buyer lives; in the Balearics the general rate is 4%. If the seller is a company, VAT applies instead. This is guidance only: check with your tax adviser.

How much do you charge to sell my boat?

I set out my fees in writing in the sale mandate, agreed before we start. They are only charged if the boat is sold to a buyer I introduced.

Do I have to give you exclusivity?

No. I work without exclusivity and you can keep your own listing in the meantime.
