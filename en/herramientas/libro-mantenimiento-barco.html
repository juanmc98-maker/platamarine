Boat maintenance log

By Juan MoranteNovember 2026

A simple log to write down everything you do to the boat: oil changes, anodes, haul-outs, the liferaft service, the insurance… I have put it together as a PDF so you can print it and keep it on board. It is free and I do not ask for your email.

Download the log as a PDF

A4 · 10 pages · to print and fill in by hand

What it is for

After a few years nobody remembers when the impeller was changed or which antifouling went on. Writing it down helps you not to skip services, to know what to ask for at the yard and not to pay twice for the same job.

And if you ever sell the boat, it is one of the first things you will be asked for. A clear history gives the buyer confidence: they can see what was done, when and by whom.

What is inside

  • Cover page with the boat details: model, year, registration, length and the engines with their serial numbers.
  • Documents and safety equipment: navigation permit, certificate of seaworthiness, ITB, insurance, liferaft, fire extinguishers, flares and EPIRB, with dates and expiry.
  • Engine log: three pages with date, hours, work done (oil and filter, fuel filter, impeller, anodes, gear oil, belts, spark plugs), workshop, cost and invoice number.
  • Hull and equipment: antifouling, anodes, through-hulls, propeller, batteries, bilge pumps, electronics and, on a sailboat, rigging and winches.
  • Laying up and launching: when the boat comes out and goes back in, and what was done each time.
  • When you sell the boat: the list of papers a buyer asks for, so you have everything to hand.

To get real use out of it

  • Write each job down on the day, even if you did it yourself.
  • Number your invoices and keep them in a folder with the same number you write in the log.
  • When a page is full, take a photo of it. If the log gets wet or lost, the history is still there.

So you do not miss a date

The log tells you when each thing expires, but you have to remember to look at it. If you would rather get a reminder before the ITB, the insurance or the liferaft service runs out, there are free reminders. And if you are closing the season, the laying-up guide covers what is worth doing before the boat sits still.
