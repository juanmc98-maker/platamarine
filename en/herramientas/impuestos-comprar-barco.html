What taxes do I pay when buying a used boat in Spain?

By Juan MoranteReviewed in September 2026

On top of the boat's price come taxes that many people only find out about after signing. Enter the price, who is selling and where the boat comes from, and I will tell you what you have to pay and how much, using the tax base the Spanish tax office applies.

The general rate for movable goods is usually 4%, but some regions charge more for larger boats. Check with your gestoría.

If the tax office values the boat above what you pay, the tax is worked out on that value.

Estimated additional taxes

–

The three taxes, in short

  • ITP: paid by the buyer when buying a boat already registered in Spain from a private owner. It depends on the region where the buyer lives; in Catalonia it is 5% and you have one month to pay it.
  • VAT: only if a company or professional sells with an invoice. In that case there is no ITP.
  • IEDMT (registration): paid once, when a boat over 8 metres is registered in Spain. A boat already under the Spanish flag that paid it back then does not pay it again.

On top of that comes the Harbour Master fee for the change of ownership (form 790, code 025). Everything is explained in paperwork and taxes and in the guide to the registration tax.

Shall I check it for a specific boat?

When you buy through me, I review the paperwork and coordinate the ITP and change of ownership with the gestoría.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the ITP on a boat in Catalonia?

5% of the boat's value, worked out on the purchase price or on the tax office table value if higher. The buyer pays it within one month of the purchase.

Do I pay ITP if I buy from a dealer?

No. If a company sells with an invoice, the sale carries VAT instead of ITP.

When is registration tax paid on a boat in Spain?

Once, when a boat over 8 metres that has not paid it here is registered in Spain, for example one bought in France. It is usually 12% of the value set by the tax authorities, unless an exemption applies.
