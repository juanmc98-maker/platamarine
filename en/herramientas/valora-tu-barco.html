Tools · Value your boat

See what affects your boat's price

By Juan MoranteSeptember 2026

Every boat is different, so I won't give you an exact figure here without seeing it: I'd be making it up. But if you answer these nine questions I'll tell you what works in favour of your price and what will bring it down, so that when we talk you already have a clear picture.

This is guidance based on your answers, not a valuation: a real asking price needs me to see the boat and its paperwork. If you like, send me the result and we'll go through it calmly.

Frequently asked questions before selling

Why don't you give me an exact price here?

A real price depends on things a form can't capture: the condition of the engine seen in person, the specific paperwork and what is actually selling right now in your bracket. This tool gives you the key points; I work out the asking price by looking at your boat and the current market, free and with no commitment.

How long does it take to sell a used boat?

It depends on the type, the length and the time of year: out of season (autumn and winter) it usually takes longer. As a rough guide, several months is normal even with a sensible price; a well-prepared listing and price can shorten it, but be wary of anyone who promises a quick sale without having seen the boat.

Can I sell if the boat isn't up to date on maintenance or paperwork?

Yes. The usual approach is to adjust the price or sort out what you can beforehand. What you shouldn't do is hide it: it tends to come out at the survey or the sea trial, and that complicates the sale more than saying it from the start.

Does this valuation cost anything?

No. Neither this tool nor the first conversation costs anything. I only charge a fee if I handle the sale and it goes through, and that is always put in writing before we start.
