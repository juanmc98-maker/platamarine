Before you finance

  • Add the purchase taxes and the yearly running costs to the price: the loan payment is not the only fixed cost.
  • Compare the APR, not just the nominal rate: the APR includes fees and shows the real cost.
  • A personal loan and a loan secured on the boat have different terms; ask about both.
  • With the boat chosen and the finance approved, you negotiate from a stronger position.

Frequently asked questions

Can you finance a used boat in Spain?

Yes. The usual options are a personal loan or a loan secured on the boat, with terms typically from 5 to 10 years depending on the lender and the boat's age.

How much deposit do lenders ask for a boat?

It depends on the lender and the boat; a deposit is common, and the older the boat, the larger it tends to be.

Does Plata Marine finance boats?

No. Plata Marine does not grant or broker loans; this tool is only for running the numbers.
