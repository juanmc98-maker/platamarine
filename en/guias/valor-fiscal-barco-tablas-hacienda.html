Tax value of a boat in Spain: the official tables and how it is calculated

By Juan MoranteUpdated October 20265 min read

When you buy a boat from a private seller in Spain, the tax isn't always calculated on what you paid. The tax authorities have their own average prices, the so-called “tables”, and it is worth knowing how they work before you sign.

What the tax value is

It is the value the authorities give a boat in order to calculate taxes. It comes from the average sale prices approved every year by the Spanish Ministry of Finance. For 2026 they are in Order HAC/1501/2025 of 17 December, in force since 1 January 2026.

They are used for transfer tax (ITP), inheritance and gift tax, and the special registration tax on certain means of transport.

How it is calculated

The Order values the hull without engine and the engines separately, then adds the two together:

  • Hull. The starting point is the value listed for that model in Annex II of the Order.
  • Engine. The starting point is a base value per unit of power, set in Annex III.
  • Years of use. Each part is multiplied by a percentage based on its years of use, using the table in Annex III: the older it is, the lower the percentage.
  • Jet skis. They are valued as a single unit, without separating hull and engine, according to their power and age.

That is why the same boat can have a different tax value depending on the engine it carries.

When it affects you

Mainly when you buy a used boat from a private seller. In that case transfer tax is paid in the buyer's region (5% in Catalonia), and the taxable base is generally the higher of the declared price and the table value. You can see it by region in the guide to transfer tax on a used boat, and run the numbers with the tax calculator.

If you buy from a company that sells with VAT, there is normally no transfer tax. In an inheritance or gift, the tax value is also used as a reference.

Tax value is not market value

The tables are averages: they ignore the boat's condition, engine hours, electronics and whether it has been impeccably maintained or not at all. A very well kept boat can sell above its tax value, and one that needs work, below it.

If you declare a price below the tables because the boat really is in worse condition, keep evidence (a survey, photos, repair quotes): the authorities can review the value and claim the difference. To find out what it is really worth on the market, use the how much is my boat worth calculator.

The “valuation tables” found online

Forums and websites share depreciation tables for used boats that are not always the current ones. The only official reference for tax purposes is the current year's Order published in the BOE (Spain's Official Gazette). If someone sends you a table, check which year it is from.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I check my boat's tax value?

In the annexes of the current year's average price Order, published in the BOE. Some regions also have their own search tool. If you are unsure about a particular model, a gestoría (administrative agent) can work it out in no time.

Do I pay transfer tax on what I paid or on the tables?

Generally on the higher of the two. If you pay more than the table value, on what you paid; if you pay less, the authorities may use the table value.

Can I use the tax value to price my boat?

No. It is for taxes. The selling price is set by the market: what similar boats in similar condition are being listed and sold for.

For guidance only, reviewed in October 2026 based on Order HAC/1501/2025 (BOE-A-2025-26357). Rules and rates may change and each Spanish region has its own: confirm your case with a gestoría or the tax office before buying or selling. We may be wrong and nothing on this page is binding.
