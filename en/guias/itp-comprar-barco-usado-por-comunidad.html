Transfer tax (ITP) when buying a used boat: what you pay in each Spanish region

By Juan MoranteUpdated September 20266 min read

When you buy a used boat from a private individual in Spain you pay the Transfer Tax (ITP). Each autonomous region sets its own rate, from 1 % in Galicia to 8 % in some regions for larger boats. Here is the full table and what you need to know to avoid surprises.

What it is and when you pay it

ITP (Impuesto sobre Transmisiones Patrimoniales Onerosas) is the tax on the purchase of used goods between private individuals. In boating it applies when you buy from a private seller a boat that is already registered in Spain. The buyer always pays it.

There are two cases where no ITP is due: when the seller is a company or a professional (the sale carries VAT instead), and when the boat is new or comes from outside Spain unregistered, which is when the registration tax comes into play.

A detail that surprises many people: the tax is paid in the region where the buyer lives, not where the boat is or where the seller lives. If you live in Madrid and buy a boat in Palma, you pay in Madrid at 4 %.

What value it is calculated on

The tax base is the higher of two values: the price in the sale contract or the table value that the Ministry of Finance publishes each year in its order of average sale prices for boats. That order assigns a value by make, model and year, with a depreciation percentage according to age.

That is why it is pointless to put a price below the real one in the contract: if the tax office sees that the table value is higher, it assesses on that value and can claim the difference with interest. If the boat is worth less than the table because of its condition, you can justify it with a survey, but it is a longer road.

Three examples

  • Buyer in Catalonia, 7.5 m boat for €60,000: 5 % of 60,000 = €3,000, paid to the Catalan tax agency within one month.
  • Buyer in the Valencia region, 8.5 m boat for €60,000: over 8 m the rate is 8 %: €4,800. The same boat at 7.99 m would pay 6 %: €3,600.
  • Buyer in Galicia, 10 m boat for €90,000: 1 % = €900, one of the lowest rates in Spain.

If the table value for that model and year were higher than the agreed price, the tax would be calculated on the table value.

How to pay it, step by step

  1. Sale contract signed by both parties, with price, date, boat details (registration, NIB, make, model, length) and both people's details.
  2. Form 620 (600 or 621 in some regions), filled in and paid online at your regional tax agency's website. You need a Spanish digital certificate or Cl@ve, or you can do it through an agency (gestoría).
  3. Deadline: generally 30 working days from signing; in Catalonia it is one month and in Andalusia two months. Late filing carries surcharges.
  4. Proof of payment: it is one of the documents the Capitanía Marítima requires for the change of ownership. Without ITP paid, the boat is not transferred to your name.

Mistakes I see often

  • Paying in the wrong region. The tax goes to the region where the buyer lives. If it is paid elsewhere, you have to request a refund and file again.
  • Declaring a low price. The tax office cross-checks the price with the tables and assesses the difference, with interest and sometimes a penalty.
  • Thinking a foreign flag exempts you. If the buyer lives in Spain, ITP is due even if the boat flies a Belgian or Polish flag.
  • Forgetting the 8 metres. In Andalusia, Asturias, Cantabria and the Valencia region the rate rises to 8 % above that length, and in Valencia also by value.
  • Leaving it to the end. The deadline runs from the signing of the contract, not from the handover of the boat.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays ITP when buying a used boat?

The buyer. The seller does not pay ITP; at most they pay income tax on the gain if they sell for more than they paid, which is unusual with boats.

Is ITP due if I buy the boat from a company?

No. If the seller is a company or a professional acting as such, the sale carries 21 % VAT and no ITP. Ask for an invoice.

Where is the ITP on a boat paid?

In the autonomous region where the buyer lives, with form 620 (or the one that region uses), usually online at its tax agency's website.

What happens if I pay ITP late?

Surcharges apply and grow with the delay (from 1 % per month up to 15 %, plus interest after a year). If the tax office requests it before you file, there may be a penalty.
