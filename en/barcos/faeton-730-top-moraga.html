My take

A 2003 Faeton 730 Top Moraga, the enclosed-wheelhouse version of the Moraga: a seaworthy pilot-boat hull, a sheltered cabin and a roomy cockpit that works just as well for a day of fishing as for a family trip to a cove.

It runs a Volvo Penta AD31 150 hp diesel with a Duoprop sterndrive, a simple, well-known engine, plus a 20 hp Honda four-stroke auxiliary fitted in 2024. The owner has it serviced every year and the next ITB inspection is due in 2029. There is no reliable hour meter; the owner estimates between 1,000 and 1,500 hours.

The Garmin fishfinder-GPS is from this summer, and the sale includes the Protender 185 tender with its 2 hp Honda, plus life jackets and flares.

The boat is in Mallorca, on a rented berth that is not part of the sale. You need a PNB licence or higher to skipper it. The price is €24,000 and viewings are by appointment.
