My take

A 2002 Sacs 535 that the owner has restored from top to bottom: new tubes, all-new upholstery, painted hull, and new wiring and batteries. Sacs is an Italian yard with a long track record in RIBs, and at 5.35 m this 535 is a very comfortable size for coves and days out with family or friends, and it can be skippered with the basic Spanish Licencia de Navegación or any higher licence.

Power comes from a Suzuki 115 hp outboard with about 400 hours and SeaStar steering. It comes with a full maintenance kit and a brand-new spare propeller that has never been used.

For everyday use it has a 50-litre water tank with shower, VHF, a stainless arch with bimini, SeaDek flooring and swim platforms. The canopy closes at the sides, with a window on each side, and has an extension that covers the whole bow, all with zips.

It is sold with a new 1,200 kg tilting multi-roller trailer with a manual and an electric winch with remote, so it is easy to move and launch. It is kept in a garage in Mallorca and can be viewed by appointment. The price is €25,000.

Equipment: New tubes, new upholstery, painted hull, new wiring and batteries, Suzuki 115 hp with SeaStar steering, 50 l water tank with shower, VHF, stainless arch, bimini with closable side panels with windows and bow extension, SeaDek, swim platforms, full maintenance kit, brand-new spare propeller, new 1,200 kg trailer with manual and electric winch.
