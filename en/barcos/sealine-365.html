How I see it

A 1989 Sealine 365 Sport Bridge with two cabins and two full heads, unusual at 11 metres, and room for ten people. A spacious, well laid-out cabin cruiser built for spending days aboard with the family, with the reassurance of twin diesels.

She carries twin Volvo Penta AQAD41 200 hp engines with approximately 1,100 hours, new turbos and batteries, and sterndrive service done in 2025. The ITB (Spanish technical survey) is passed and valid until December 2030. The owner reports moderate fuel consumption for her size.

Electronics include a Garmin GPS/plotter with updated charts and sounder, plus a new autopilot. Interior upholstery was renewed in 2022 and the exterior canvas in 2024. Extended bathing platform and deck shower. Delivered with fresh antifouling.

Asking price is €75,000. She lies on the Costa Daurada (Tarragona), privately owned under Spanish flag (7th list) with taxes paid. Viewings without obligation; mechanical check and sea trial available.
