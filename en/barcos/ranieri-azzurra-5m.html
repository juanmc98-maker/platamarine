How I see it

A very well kept 5-metre open boat, dry-stored, ideal for cruising, swimming and inshore fishing. Easy to handle and cheap to maintain; it's towed on a trailer with no need for a mooring.

Johnson 60 hp four-stroke engine with approximately 400 hours and a recent service. Certified for 6 people.

Equipment: bimini, bow sundeck, depth sounder, swim ladder and built-in fuel tank.

She was on the Costa Daurada, with an optional trailer. Ownership-transfer costs not included.
