How I see it

A 2008 Beneteau Monte Carlo 27: a sporty cuddy-cabin open with a red hull and a big cockpit for her size. Built for day trips with the option of a comfortable night or two aboard. She has a forward saloon that converts into a double berth, an aft cabin under the cockpit, heads with hot water and a galley.

Power is a Volvo Penta 5.7 GXi 350 hp petrol with DPS sterndrive and around 650 hours. The owner reports about 20 knots cruising and 27 top. Bow thruster fitted, with maintenance invoices from 2022 onwards.

The Raymarine Axiom 7 sounder is new, with a transom transducer. The stereo and speakers are new too, and the cushions have been renewed. Galley with 220 V, 12 V fridge, bimini, camper canopy and winter cover.

Worth knowing: the bow thruster has a broken shear key (repair pending, estimated at around €150) and the fridge's internal freezer cannot be used: its lid broke and it ices up so it will not open. Otherwise she shows the normal wear of an 18-year-old boat.

Asking price is €64,900. She is laid up ashore on the Costa Brava, Spanish flag, privately owned. Viewings by appointment, with some notice.
