How I see it

A 2002 Rodman 900 Fly, a cabin motor boat with a flybridge. You can helm it from the lower station or from the flybridge.

It has twin Volvo Penta KAMD300 inboard diesels of 202 kW each (about 275 hp). The hours are confirmed with the photo of the hour meter and the papers. The owner has the maintenance invoices; I check them before reserving.

Inside there is the forward cabin with a double bed, a second cabin with a bed and a bathroom with washbasin and toilet. The interior is finished in wood, as the photos show.

It is on the Costa Brava (Girona) and the berth is not part of the sale. According to the papers it is 9.00 m long and 3.31 m wide, so you need the Spanish PER licence or higher. The price is €59,990. It is a private sale, so the buyer normally pays the transfer tax (ITP) of their region; the boat's VAT status is confirmed with the papers.

Before reserving, I would do a sea trial and an independent survey.
