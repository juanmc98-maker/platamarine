My take

A 2004 Starfisher 840 R, a fishing-cruiser with an enclosed wheelhouse, a roomy cockpit and a swim platform. She works just as well for a day's fishing as for a family day at anchor, and the wheelhouse keeps you sheltered on cooler days.

She has twin Yanmar 200 hp diesels. The engines were serviced in 2025 (turbos, anodes and intercooler) and the propellers are Prospeed-coated. The owner reports a fuel burn of around 11.5 litres per hour.

Electronics are complete: two 10" Furuno displays with radar, chartplotter and sounder, autopilot and VHF DSC. On deck there is an electric windlass, trim tabs and a transom shower, and for fishing she has a trolling valve and four sockets for electric reels.

Below, a saloon that converts into a double berth, a galley with sink and fridge, full heads with WC and shower, a forward cabin and a TV.

She lies in Galicia on a rented berth that is not part of the sale. Skippering her requires the Spanish PER or a higher licence. The price is €50,900 and viewings are by appointment.

Equipment: Twin Yanmar 200 hp diesels, engines serviced 2025, Prospeed-coated propellers, two 10" Furuno displays with radar, chartplotter and sounder, autopilot, VHF DSC, radio-CD with cockpit speakers, electric windlass, trim tabs, transom shower, swim platform, trolling valve, 4 sockets for electric reels, convertible saloon, galley with sink, fridge, heads with WC and shower, forward cabin, TV.
