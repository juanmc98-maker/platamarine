How I see it

A 2008 Beneteau Oceanis 50 with one owner from new. It is a large, very well equipped yacht built for spending long periods on board: generator, air conditioning and watermaker, which is what lets you live aboard without depending on the marina.

It has a Yanmar 110 hp engine with a 3-blade Gori folding propeller and a bow thruster for manoeuvring. The mast, boom and standing rigging date from 2019. For short-handed sailing it has three electric winches and two autopilots, and the electronics are complete.

Worth knowing: the deck teak is in fair condition and will need work, which is already reflected in the price. The owner uses one of the heads as a workshop, but keeps all the new parts stored to refit it (electric toilet, mirror, grey-water tank). The engine has no hour meter.

The price is €155,000. She is in La Pobla de Farnals (Valencia), on a rented berth. The RIB tender with its 9.8 hp outboard is included. Viewings by appointment.

Equipment: Yanmar 110 hp, 3-blade Gori folding propeller, bow thruster, mast, boom and standing rigging from 2019, Paguro 6500 generator, air conditioning, Desalator Duo watermaker 100 l/h, three electric winches, two autopilots, full electronics, solar panels, sprayhood and bimini, RIB tender with 9.8 hp outboard.
