How I see it

A 2008 Lexsia XS20, a 5.84 m outboard boat with a cabin. What makes it stand out: a bow cabin to stow gear or get out of the sun, a transom shower, two chartplotters with sounder and an electric windlass with remote control for effortless anchoring. Roomy cockpit and bow sunbed: a comfortable day boat for trips to coves, easy to handle and to maintain.

It has a Suzuki DF115 four-stroke with about 500 hours and servicing up to date. It has had three owners.

It is very well equipped: electric windlass with remote, two chartplotters (Garmin GPSMAP 922xs and Humminbird Helix 5), fixed VHF, two batteries with a parallel switch, automatic bilge pump and transom shower. The cockpit floor is gelcoat, the cockpit upholstery is new, and it has a bimini, anchoring cover, cockpit courtesy lights and navigation lights. The Simrad autopilot is currently not working. It also has a swim ladder, anchor, fenders and a fresh water tank.

It is licensed for 6 people, exempt from the ITB inspection and can be skippered with the Navigation Licence or a higher licence. It has a rented berth in Blanes that may be transferred. The price is €22,990; it is a private sale, so there is no VAT and the buyer pays the transfer tax (ITP) of their region. Spanish flag, 7th list (private use), with VAT and registration tax paid; sold without a trailer.
