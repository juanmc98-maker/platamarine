My take

A 2015 Tiger Marine Top Line 650, a RIB of about 6.5 m with a centre console, very comfortable for trips to the coves and days out with family or friends. It is in very good condition: the owner only keeps it in the water in August and stores it indoors for the rest of the year.

Power comes from a 2012 Suzuki DF150 150 hp outboard with about 110 hours, very few for its age. It is a fuel-injected four-stroke with plenty of power for this hull.

On board there is a centre console with windscreen and a helm seat with storage, a stern bench, a bow area with cushions and a table, a stern arch with bimini and swim platforms with teak-style decking.

It is sold with its trailer, so it can be kept ashore and taken wherever you want to go boating. The berth is not included. It is on the Costa Daurada (Tarragona), under the Polish flag, and you need the Spanish PNB licence or higher to skipper it. The price is €33,000 and it can be viewed by appointment.
