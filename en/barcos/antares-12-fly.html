My take

A 2024 Beneteau Antares 12 Fly with 110 hours and the equipment that makes the difference on a boat this size: twin 400 hp Mercury outboards with joystick for full control in harbour, a Seakeeper gyro stabiliser for comfort at anchor, a generator and air conditioning.

It has 3 cabins and 2 heads, twin helm stations (lower and flybridge), a side terrace, a hydraulic passerelle and natural teak in the cockpit. It comes with full electronics, a stern camera, underwater lights and zone 2 safety equipment including a liferaft. VAT and registration tax have already been paid in Spain.

It was ashore on the Costa Daurada (Tarragona). The berth was not included in the sale. You need the Spanish PER licence or higher to skipper it.
