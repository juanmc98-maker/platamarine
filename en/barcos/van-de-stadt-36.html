How I see it

A 1981 Van de Stadt Zeehond 36: a Dutch-designed steel sailing yacht built for proper passage-making, with a bright, well-kept varnished-wood interior. The kind of boat you buy to log miles, not to sit in the marina.

The owner has gone through her thoroughly: the hull has been sandblasted and painted with three coats of anti-corrosion epoxy and two of Hempel, the sails are in perfect condition and the interior upholstery is brand new. Power is a Solé Mini 62 hp with around 1,000 hours and a Twin Disc twin-plate gearbox. On deck: synthetic teak, electric windlass and wheel steering with the instruments in view.

She is sloop-rigged with a removable staysail, which can be set up on a furler if wanted. Certified for 10 people, with an insured value of €88,000.

Asking price is €72,000 and the owner will listen to offers. She is berthed in Alicante on a monthly rented berth, Spanish flag, privately owned. The owner offers delivery to any port on the Spanish Mediterranean coast on completion, and a car or a smaller boat in part exchange can be considered. Viewings by appointment.

Equipment: refitted steel hull (sandblasted, 3 coats epoxy + 2 Hempel), sails in perfect condition, removable staysail, Solé Mini 62 hp with Twin Disc gearbox, electric windlass, wheel steering, synthetic teak deck, blue sprayhood and covers, brand-new upholstery, galley with sink, marine heads.
