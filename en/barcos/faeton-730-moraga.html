How I see it

A 2005 Faeton 730 Moraga built for safe cruising and fishing: a very seaworthy pilot-boat hull, with a wraparound windscreen and hardtop. The Volvo Penta KAD32P 170 hp engine has approximately 1,300 hours and had a full service with engine removal in December 2023, with maintenance records available.

Well equipped for fishing: a live-bait well with saltwater circulation, rod holders spread across the deck, two outlets for an electric reel and a 12V Italwinch electric windlass. On the electronics side it carries a Raymarine ES95 with GPS and fishfinder, Raymarine Quantum radar, a p70R autopilot and, separately, a Furuno FCV-588 fishfinder fitted in 2021.

The asking price is €28,900. With the Minn Kota Terrova electric bow thruster visible fitted in the photos, the deal is €32,900.

Located in Mallorca. Mooring not included in the deal. Can be viewed with no obligation.
