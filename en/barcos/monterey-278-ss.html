How I see it

A day-cruiser sportboat with a cabin, one of the most sought-after in this length range: 8.40 m, well under what requires a large berth, with a spacious cockpit and an extended swim platform that makes it very comfortable at anchor.

What matters most on this unit is how lightly it has been used: 350 hours on a 2011 boat, dry-stored every winter. The Volvo Penta 5.7 GXi V8 with DuoProp leg is a well-known set-up with a good network of workshops in Catalonia.

Equipped to spend the day without a worry: radar arch with awnings, convertible bow sundeck, wet bar with sink, separate heads with marine WC, Garmin plotter, VHF with DSC, Bennett trim tabs and 220 V shore power hook-up with charger.
