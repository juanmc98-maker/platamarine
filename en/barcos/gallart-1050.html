My take

A 1977 Gallart 10.50 Fly, a classic with a flybridge, twin helm stations and a teak-floored cockpit with a table. It is a boat for spending days and nights on board with the family on a very modest budget for ten and a half metres.

It has twin 192 hp Volvo Penta diesels. The starboard engine was renewed a few years ago and has roughly half the hours of the port one. The owner has the boatyard invoices from 2021 to 2023 covering the mechanical and maintenance work.

Below there is a saloon with galley, a forward cabin, bunks and heads with WC and washbasin.

It spends May to October in the water on the Costa Brava and the rest of the year ashore, on a rented berth that is not included in the sale. It is under the Polish flag and you need the Spanish PER licence or higher to skipper it. The price is €29,900 and it can be viewed by appointment.

Equipment: Twin Volvo Penta 192 hp diesels, flybridge, lower helm, windlass, teak-floored cockpit with table, saloon with galley, forward cabin, bunks, heads with WC and washbasin, maintenance invoices from 2021 to 2023.
