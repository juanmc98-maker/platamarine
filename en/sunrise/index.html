New boats

Sunrise: new boats from 4 to 7 metres

Simple, easy-to-maintain boats built in Poland by Sunrise Yacht. They are ordered from the factory and I help you choose the model, engine and equipment. I am their dealer in Catalonia.

  • Built in Poland
  • CE design category: C (certified by PRS)
  • Delivery: 4 to 12 weeks depending on the model
  • Warranty: 3 years from delivery
  • Payment: 40% when ordering and the rest when the boat is ready for transport
  • Transport to Barcelona: about €2,000 per boat, extra
