For boat builders

Do you build boats and have no one representing you in Catalonia yet?

Juan MorantePlata Marine · Catalonia

I’m Juan Morante, a yacht broker in Catalonia. I’m looking for boat builders with no dealer here yet, to represent them as a commission-based sales agent: I introduce your boats to local buyers and see each sale through to signing.

How I work

  • Commission-based sales agent. I work under an agency agreement (Spanish Law 12/1992) and am only paid on closed sales.
  • No stock or demo boat on my side. Sea trials are arranged with the yard or with a client’s boat whose owner is happy to show it.
  • Orders and invoices are issued by the yard. I present the brand, handle enquiries, accompany viewings and take each deal through to signing.
  • Same list price. The client pays the same as buying direct from the factory.

What I can offer

  • 12 years in customer-facing sales, first in vehicle sales and now as a yacht broker, listening to what each client needs.
  • A website, www.platamarine.com, in four languages, with guides, calculators and boats for sale read by buyers in Catalonia and the Balearic Islands.
  • Service in Spanish, Catalan and English (French with a translator).
  • Knowledge of local buyers: which lengths and types of boat sell on the Costa Brava, the Maresme, Barcelona and the Costa Daurada.

Which yards I’m a good fit for

  • No official dealer in Catalonia (if you already have one, I don’t want to step on anyone’s toes).
  • CE marking with a verifiable notified body.
  • Motor boats of any length and type; sailboats, case by case.
  • Willing to share technical sheets, photos, price lists and real delivery times.

We usually start with a call or an email to get to know each other, look at your range and check whether it fits what buyers here are looking for. If it does, we put it in writing: territory, commission, payment terms and how the clients I introduce are registered.
