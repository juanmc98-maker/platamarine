Which jet ski should you buy for your use?

To get started or ride now and then

Sea-Doo Spark and Spark X: light and easy to ride and tow. If you want something more playful, the Spark Trixx.

For the family and summer

Sea-Doo GTI 130 and GTI SE 170, or Kawasaki STX 160: three seats, stable and comfortable for rides near the coast.

For long rides two-up

Sea-Doo GTX 170 and GTX 230, or Kawasaki Ultra 160LX: bigger seat, more stability and a larger fuel tank.

Maximum comfort and power

Sea-Doo GTX Limited 350 and Kawasaki Ultra 310LX: each brand's most complete equipment for those who ride often and far.

Sporty

Sea-Doo RXP-X 350 and RXT-X 350, Kawasaki STX 310R: built to accelerate and turn hard. Not the most comfortable for cruising.

Skiing, wake, fishing and touring

Sea-Doo Wake 170 and Wake Pro 230 or Kawasaki Ultra 160LX-S Wakesport for towing; Fish Pro Trophy 170 for fishing; Explorer Pro 230 for long trips.

FAQ

Why is the price on request?

Because it changes with version, colour, extras and current promotions. I prefer to give you a closed figure in writing, detailing what it includes.

What licence do I need?

The Spanish Licencia de Navegación (LN) or higher. There is a summary in boating licences.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the model, colour and available units. Delivery time is on request and I confirm it in writing before you decide.

Do you have used jet skis?

Yes, when there are some in the portfolio. See used jet skis or tell me what you are looking for and I will let you know.

What costs are there besides the price?

Registration, third-party liability insurance (mandatory), a trailer if you will tow it yourself, yearly servicing and storage. I detail it in the quote so there are no surprises. If you need a trailer, see the Goliath trailers.

Are you an official dealer?

No. I help you choose and handle the order with an official dealer of the brand. The written quote states who invoices and delivers the jet ski, and the warranty is the manufacturer's.
