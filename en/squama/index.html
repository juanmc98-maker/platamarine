Squama 21

New boat, built to order6.40 mOpen or SundeckMercury engine included

From €56,000+ taxeswith Mercury 115 · transport extra

A 6.40 m day boat with a stepped hull, centre console and a design that looks like no other. It is built to order by Pičuljan Pro-Nautic on the island of Rab (Croatia): colours, upholstery, decking and equipment are chosen for each boat. I help you configure it and bring it over. Builder's photos; they may show optional equipment.

Open or Sundeck

Same hull, two bows

Squama 21 Open

Open bow with a bench and storage that converts into a sunbed. The version for more people outside and moving around the whole boat.

Squama 21 Sundeck

Closed bow with a deck: underneath there is an Alcantara-upholstered bed with storage, to take shelter, get changed or stow your things. A deck window is available as an option.

Engine

Mercury outboard, your choice

The price includes the engine fitted, with hydraulic steering, throttle, propeller and gauges. These are the four Mercury options and the boat price with each one, with standard equipment:

  • Mercury FourStroke 115 CT€56,000+ taxes

Prices excluding VAT. Other engine brands on request. According to the builder, it exceeds 45 knots; speed depends on engine, load and sea conditions.

How to buy it

  1. Tell me which version, engine and options you like.
  2. I send you a written quote: boat, engine, options, transport and taxes.
  3. You sign the order with the builder, pay the deposit set in the contract and production starts.
  4. Transport, registration and handover in Spain.

How I see it

I see it for day trips to coves with family or friends, up to 7 people. If you will mostly go as a couple or want to sunbathe at the bow, the Open; if you prefer a covered spot to stow things or lie down for a while, the Sundeck. In Spain you need the PNB or a higher licence to skipper it.
