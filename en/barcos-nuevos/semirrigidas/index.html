How to choose

Length and licence

In Spain, up to 8 m a PNB is enough; from 8 to 15 m you need the PER. A 7–8 m RIB already carries 8–10 people comfortably for the day.

Open or with a cabin

Open RIBs give you more cockpit and sunpad. A cabin gives you somewhere to change, store gear or spend a night, at the cost of some deck space.

Engine and budget

The engine can be a large part of the final price. On each model I show whether the “from” price includes it, and we compare engine options before requesting the quote.

How buying a new boat works

  1. You tell me what you are looking for: type of boat, use, area and budget.
  2. We choose the model, engine and options, comparing brands if needed.
  3. I send you a written quote: boat, options, transport and delivery time, which is always confirmed with the shipyard.
  4. You sign under each shipyard’s payment terms, always in writing before paying anything.
  5. The shipyard builds the boat and I follow it with you until delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Why a RIB rather than a day boat?

The tubes give stability, safety and a softer ride in choppy seas, and make berthing less stressful. On the other hand, they take up some beam for the same interior space.

What maintenance do the tubes need?

Cleaning with suitable products, protecting them from the sun when the boat is not in use and checking the pressure. Well looked after, they last many years; the shipyard states the warranty for each material.

Can large RIBs be towed on a trailer?

It depends on weight and beam. Up to about 7.5 m it is usually feasible with the right trailer and car; above that, a berth or dry storage is the norm.
