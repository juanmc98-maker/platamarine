How to choose

How many people and what for

For swimming and anchoring with family or friends, prioritise cockpit, sunpad and shade. For longer trips, look more at helm protection and the hull.

Where you will keep it

Boats up to about 6.5 m are easy to trailer and suit dry storage. Larger ones usually need a berth: factor in that cost from the start.

GRP or aluminium

GRP is the most common and affordable. Aluminium (Brizo) is tougher and lighter, but the boat costs more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a day boat and a RIB?

A day boat has a rigid hull without tubes: more usable deck space for the same length and a more classic look. A RIB is more stable at rest and more forgiving when berthing.

Which licence do I need for a 6 m boat in Spain?

As a guide, up to 6 m the Licencia de Navegación is enough (within the power and area limits set by the rules). From 6 to 8 m, the PNB. Each model shows the minimum licence.

Can I use it only in summer?

Yes. Many day boats spend the winter in dry storage or at home on a trailer, which keeps maintenance and berthing costs down.
