How buying a new boat works

  1. You tell me what you are looking for: type of boat, use, area and budget.
  2. We choose the model, engine and options, comparing brands if needed.
  3. I send you a written quote: boat, options, transport and delivery time, which is always confirmed with the shipyard.
  4. You sign under each shipyard’s payment terms, always in writing before paying anything.
  5. The shipyard builds the boat and I follow it with you until delivery.

Frequently asked questions

What does the “from” price include?

It is the starting price from each shipyard’s price list, excluding taxes. Depending on the brand it is with or without engines (shown on each model). It does not include options, transport, registration or, where applicable, the Spanish registration tax (IEDMT). The final price is the one in the written quote.

Which taxes do I need to add?

In Spain, usually VAT (21%) and, for pleasure boats over 8 m in length, the special registration tax (IEDMT). It depends on the boat and your situation: this is a guide only and should be confirmed with your adviser.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the model, the configuration and the shipyard’s production. Delivery time is always on request and I confirm it in writing before you sign.

Which licence do I need?

It depends on the length and use. As a guide, for Spanish licences: Licencia de Navegación up to 6 m, PNB up to 8 m, PER up to 15 m (24 m with the extra endorsement), Patrón or Capitán de Yate up to 24 m and, over 24 m, a professional licence. Each model page shows the minimum licence.

Who do I pay and who gives the warranty?

Each shipyard has its own payment terms and manufacturer’s warranty. Before you pay anything you will have in writing who invoices, how payment works and what the warranty covers.

Can I see the boat before buying?

It depends on the brand and model. When possible, we arrange a visit to the same model or a test at the shipyard or at a boat show.
