New boats

SeaStorm: polyethylene boats from 3.7 to 5.2 metres

Small one-piece polyethylene (HDPE) boats built in Estonia by SeaStorm. They are light, need very little maintenance and are easy to take close to the beach. They are ordered from the factory and I help you choose the model and engine. I am their dealer in Catalonia.

  • Built in Estonia from one-piece polyethylene (HDPE). Brand present in 75 countries
  • CE design category: C (and D with more people on board)
  • Built to order: indicative lead time, confirmed in the quote
  • Shipping from Estonia not included in the price: indicative, about €2,090 for a shared load (e.g. a 17 and a 13) and about €4,390 for a full truck
  • Prices for the Advantage configuration: console with steering wheel, windscreen, cushions with backrests, side rails and motor plate
  • Double hull, self-draining, shallow draught (18 to 22 cm)
