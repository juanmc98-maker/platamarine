Used Jeanneau Cap Camarat 8.5 WA: what to check, prices and who it's for

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed September 2026

The big brother of the 7.5 WA and the best-selling walkaround in its size class: almost three metres of beam, a cabin with a proper heads, a galley in the cockpit and a category B hull that takes on the Mediterranean when the wind picks up. It's the boat for someone who wants to reach the Balearics in a day and go fishing with five friends the next. It costs double a 7.5, and it shows in every detail.

What to check on a used Cap Camarat 8.5 WA

Beyond the general points in the guide on what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Two engines: double the maintenance; ask for both service histories and check the hours match up between them.
  • Hull over 8 metres: if the boat already paid registration tax in Spain, it isn't paid again; if it comes from France, factor in the 12%.
  • Bathing platforms and ladder, as on the 7.5; and on this model, the outdoor galley's water fittings.
  • Series 2 (2018 onwards) improved the cabin and the console; listings don't always state the series.
  • Electronics: on a boat at this price, a large plotter and radar are standard; a boat with none of that means adding €5,000 to the price.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Above 8 metres you need the PER licence, and if the boat comes from abroad, factor in the 12% registration tax (see the IEDMT guide). If it's already registered in Spain it isn't paid again. When you buy it you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I detail it in the guide on paperwork and ownership transfer. A 9-metre berth on the Catalan coast runs roughly €3,000 to €5,000 a year depending on the marina; rates in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

Jeanneau Cap Camarat 7.5 WA (much the same, a metre smaller and half the price); Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck (if the use is more beach than fishing). And if you're not sure what type of boat you need, the two-minute test will help.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Jeanneau Cap Camarat 8.5 WA cost?

It mostly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2011 to 2014: €65,000 to €76,000; 2015 to 2019: €84,000 to €107,000; 2020 onwards: from €120,000. These are guide ranges taken from listings on European portals in September 2026; the real price is settled by inspecting the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Jeanneau Cap Camarat 8.5 WA?

At around 8.4 metres you need the PER licence (up to 15 metres and 12 miles from the coast); the PNB licence stops at 8 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Jeanneau Cap Camarat 8.5 WA?

If it's already registered in Spain, no: the tax is paid only once. If you bring it from France or Italy, once it's over 8 metres you owe 12% of the value before registering it. Buying a Spanish used boat means paying transfer tax (ITP) and the Harbour Master's fee.
