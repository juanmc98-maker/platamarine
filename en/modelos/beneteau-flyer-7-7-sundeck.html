Used Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck: what to check, prices and who it suits

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed September 2026

The "big" sundeck under 8 metres: a huge sunbathing area, a cockpit with an outdoor galley, and a cabin that comfortably fits two adults with two children in the saloon. It's the boat for people who spend the whole day aboard with six or eight people and want to sleep the odd night in a cove. Beneteau's Air Step hull runs very flat and dry for its size. One thing to watch: at 2.3 tonnes plus engine, forget about trailering it.

What to check on a used Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck

Besides the general points in the guide to what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • The Air Step has hull air intakes: check they're clean and crack-free; a boatyard fitter can check this in ten minutes.
  • Outdoor galley and fridge: the equipment that fails most; check the fridge cools and the hob lights.
  • Sunbathing area upholstery: a large surface, expensive to replace.
  • With the Suzuki DF250 it's a very fast boat; with 200hp it's fine but tight with ten people aboard.
  • Many have lived in France: get the import history and confirm the re-flagging is complete (see the paperwork guide).

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Under 8 metres there's no registration tax on a boat already registered in Spain, and either the PNB or the PER is enough to skipper it. When buying you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I cover this in the guide to paperwork and change of ownership. Mooring for a boat this length runs €2,500 to 4,000 a year on the Catalan coast depending on the marina, and considerably less on dry storage; rates are in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

The Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck (the same idea, smaller and cheaper); the Jeanneau Cap Camarat 7.5 WA (more seaworthy, less sunbathing space). And if you're not sure which type of boat suits you, the two-minute test will clarify it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck cost?

It mainly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2015 to 2016: €41,000 to 59,000; 2017 to 2018: €54,000 to 72,500; the newer model (Flyer 8 SUNdeck): from around €95,000 with engine. These are indicative ranges from European listings in September 2026; the real price is settled by viewing the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck?

Under 8 metres the PNB is enough (up to 5 nm from a shelter), and with the PER you can sail up to 12 nm and to the Balearics with the relevant extension. The Navigation Licence doesn't reach: it's capped at 6 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck?

No, if it's already registered in Spain: the tax is paid only once. When you buy it, you do pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the change-of-ownership fee at the Harbour Master's Office.
