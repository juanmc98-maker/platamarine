Used Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck: what to check, prices and who it's for

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed September 2026

This is the archetypal entry-level family boat in Catalonia: a sunpad up front, a cockpit with a table behind, and a berth to change in or sleep a summer night. With 150 hp it has plenty for the beach and coves. Its big advantage is price and how easily it sells on afterwards; its limit is that it isn't a boat for rough seas or serious fishing.

What to check on a used Activ 675 Sundeck

Beyond the general points in the guide on what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Mercury 150 EFI: a simple, reliable engine, but ask for annual servicing; if the seller has no invoices, negotiate as if the major service is due.
  • Condition of the sunpad cushions and the bow cover: they fade and crack in the sun, and a full new set runs over €2,000.
  • Bilge and the windlass motor cover at the bow: water gets in easily if the seals are old.
  • This model being a former rental boat is common: not a problem in itself, but check the hours and deck wear.
  • Date of the last ITB inspection if it's registered on List 6 (charter): I explain it in the ITB guide.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Under 8 metres there's no registration tax on a boat already registered in Spain, and skippering it only needs the PNB or PER licence. When you buy it you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I detail it in the guide on paperwork and ownership transfer. For mooring, a boat this size costs between €2,500 and €4,000 a year on the Catalan coast depending on the marina, and considerably less in dry storage; rates in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

Jeanneau Cap Camarat 6.5 CC (a more sea-capable open boat, no cabin); Beneteau Flyer 7.7 SUNdeck (the same concept a metre bigger). And if you're not sure what type of boat you need, the two-minute test will help.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck cost?

It mostly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2011 to 2016: €25,000 to €32,000; 2017 to 2020: €36,000 to €45,000; 2021 onwards: €45,000 to €55,000 (new with 150 hp is around €52,000). These are guide ranges taken from listings on European portals in September 2026; the real price is settled by inspecting the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck?

Under 8 metres, the PNB licence is enough (up to 5 miles from a safe haven), and the PER licence lets you sail up to 12 miles and to the Balearics with the relevant add-on. A Navigation Licence isn't enough: its limit is 6 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck?

No, if it's already registered in Spain: the tax is paid only once. Buying it does mean paying transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's ownership-change fee.
