Used Quicksilver Activ 605 Open: what to check, prices and who it's for

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed October 2026

A centre-console open of just over 6 metres for day trips, the beach and a swim. The current version is 6.46 m overall and 5.75 m hull length according to the manufacturer; for the licence, what counts is the length stated in each boat's papers. If it is 6 metres or less, the basic Navigation Licence is enough (daytime only and no more than 2 miles out); to go further you need the PNB or PER. It has no cabin: it is not a boat for rough seas or for sleeping aboard.

Quick specs

There are two generations with the same name: the previous one (built until 2022, according to published spec sheets) and the current one, on sale since 2023 and longer. What to check on a used Activ 605 Open

Beyond the general points in the guide on what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Mercury 100-115 EFI: ask for annual service invoices; without a history, budget for a full service right after buying it.
  • Trailer: with the boat, engine and fuel tank the rig is well over 750 kg, so the trailer needs its own registration and inspection (ITV), and your car and driving licence must allow that weight (check the maximum towable mass in the car's documents). If one is included, check the tyres, lights and paperwork.
  • Ask whether it has been a rental boat: that need not be a problem if the hours, maintenance and deck condition are reasonable.
  • Upholstery and bimini top: this is the first thing to age and the biggest factor in price.
  • Length in the papers: this is what decides the licence. Check the length on that boat's registration certificate: 6 metres or less, the basic Navigation Licence is valid; otherwise, PNB or PER.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Under 8 metres there's no registration tax on a boat already registered in Spain, and the licence you need depends on the length stated in its papers: 6 metres or less, the basic Navigation Licence is enough; otherwise, the PNB or PER. When you buy it you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I detail it in the guide on paperwork and ownership transfer. For mooring, a boat this size costs between €2,500 and €4,000 a year on the Catalan coast depending on the marina, and considerably less in dry storage; rates in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

Beneteau Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck (half a metre more and better equipped); Jeanneau Cap Camarat 6.5 CC (a more sea-capable open boat for fishing). And if you're not sure what type of boat you need, the two-minute test will help.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Quicksilver Activ 605 Open cost?

It mostly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2016 to 2021: €20,000 to €27,000; 2022 to 2023: €24,000 to €36,000; 2024 onwards: €33,000 to €48,000 (new with 115 hp is around €40,000). These are guide ranges taken from listings on European portals in October 2026; the real price is settled by inspecting the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Quicksilver Activ 605 Open?

According to the manufacturer, the current version is 6.46 m overall and 5.75 m hull length. For the licence, what counts is the length stated in the boat's papers: 6 metres or less, the basic Navigation Licence is enough (daytime, up to 2 miles); with the PNB you can go up to 5 miles from a shelter and with the PER, 12. Check it on your boat's registration certificate.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Quicksilver Activ 605 Open?

No, if it's already registered in Spain: the tax is paid only once. Buying it does mean paying transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's ownership-change fee.
