Used Beneteau Antares 8: what to check, prices and who it suits

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed October 2026

The direct rival to the Merry Fisher 795, with one strong argument: two cabins in 8 metres, one forward and one under the cockpit, so a family with two children can sleep aboard. Enclosed helm, side door, galley and heads: a boat for year-round cruising and fishing. It's one of the fastest-selling models in Catalonia because it's chased by the same buyers who want a Merry Fisher.

What to check on a used Antares 8

Besides the general points in the guide to what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Check the exact hull length on the registration document: depending on the version it's around 8 metres, which affects registration tax on an imported boat and sometimes the mooring price.
  • Side windows and door: seals and runners, as on any pilothouse; a stain on the headliner means water's getting in.
  • With twin 150hp outboards there's more maintenance and fuel use; with a single 250hp it's simpler and does the job just as well for this use.
  • Aft cabin: check for damp and smell, it's the least ventilated area.
  • Many come from France, where it sells very well: ask for the full history of the re-flagging and VAT status.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Under 8 metres there's no registration tax on a boat already registered in Spain, and either the PNB or the PER is enough to skipper it. When buying you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I cover this in the guide to paperwork and change of ownership. Mooring for a boat this length runs €2,500 to 4,000 a year on the Catalan coast depending on the marina, and considerably less on dry storage; rates are in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

The Jeanneau Merry Fisher 795 (the rival, one cabin but more light); the Bavaria 27 Sport (if you'd rather have an inboard and an open cabin). And if you're not sure which type of boat suits you, the two-minute test will clarify it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Beneteau Antares 8 cost?

It mainly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2016 to 2018: €45,000 to 73,000; 2019 to 2021: €67,000 to 100,000 (wide spread depending on engine and kit); 2022 onward: €80,000 to 110,000. These are indicative ranges from European listings in October 2026; the real price is settled by viewing the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Beneteau Antares 8?

Under 8 metres the PNB is enough (up to 5 nm from a shelter), and with the PER you can sail up to 12 nm and to the Balearics with the relevant extension. The Navigation Licence doesn't reach: it's capped at 6 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Beneteau Antares 8?

No, if it's already registered in Spain: the tax is paid only once. When you buy it, you do pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the change-of-ownership fee at the Harbour Master's Office.
