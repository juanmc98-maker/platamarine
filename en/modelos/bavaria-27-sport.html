Used Bavaria 27 Sport: what to check, prices and who it suits

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed September 2026

A German 8.5-metre cabin cruiser with a lot of boat for the money: a cabin you can actually sleep in, a separate heads, and a big cockpit for daytime use. It sold well across the Mediterranean and today it's one of the cheapest 8-metre cruisers per metre of length. It suits weekends aboard with two to four people and day trips with more. One thing to watch: past 8 metres you need to check the registration tax if it's coming in from abroad.

What to check on a used 27 Sport

Besides the general points in the guide to what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Inboard engine with sterndrive: check the bellows, anodes, sterndrive oil changes and whether it's been stored dry over winter; a neglected sterndrive costs more than the price difference between two boats.
  • Petrol or diesel version: the D3 diesel uses less fuel and resells better, but is more expensive to maintain; the 5.0 GXi petrol is simple with a good network of workshops.
  • Gelcoat and osmosis: this is a boat over 12 years old; ask for the date of the last antifouling and inspect the hull hauled out.
  • Covers and upholstery: they age on Bavarias; a new set runs over €3,000.
  • Registration tax: not due if it was already registered in Spain; if it's coming from France or Italy, budget 12% for going over 8 metres.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Past 8 metres, you need the PER licence, and if the boat is coming from abroad, budget for the 12% registration tax (see the IEDMT guide). If it's already registered in Spain, it isn't paid again. When buying you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I cover this in the guide to paperwork and change of ownership. A mooring for a 9-metre boat on the Catalan coast runs €3,000 to 5,000 a year depending on the marina; rates in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

The Beneteau Antares 8 (more seaworthy and enclosed, outboard-powered); the Jeanneau Merry Fisher 795 (a pilothouse under 8 metres). And if you're not sure which type of boat suits you, the two-minute test will clarify it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Bavaria 27 Sport cost?

It mainly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2006 to 2008: €35,000 to 58,000 depending on engine and condition; 2009 to 2012: €50,000 to 67,000. These are indicative ranges from European listings in September 2026; the real price is settled by viewing the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Bavaria 27 Sport?

At around 8.5 metres you need the PER licence (up to 15 metres and 12 nm from the coast); the PNB tops out at 8 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Bavaria 27 Sport?

If it's already registered in Spain, no: it's paid only once. If you bring it in from France or Italy, going over 8 metres means 12% of the value before you can register it. Buying a Spanish-registered used boat, you pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee.
