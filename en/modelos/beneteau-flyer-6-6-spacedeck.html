Used Beneteau Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck: what to check, prices and who it suits

By Juan MorantePrices reviewed September 2026

An open of nearly 7 metres with Beneteau's Air Step hull, which runs flatter and drier than most opens its size. Big cockpit, convertible seating and a console with an optional heads: a day boat for six that also works for fishing. It sold well between 2015 and 2019, and today it's a very reasonable buy in the €30,000 bracket.

What to check on a used Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck

Besides the general points in the guide to what to check when buying a used boat, on this model I focus on this:

  • Air Step hull: check that the hull's air intakes are clean; obvious in two minutes hauled out.
  • With 115hp it's tight for six people; 150hp is the sweet spot; 200hp is more than enough.
  • Forward cushions and bimini: expensive to replace, and what wears fastest in the sun.
  • Many come from France (a big seller there): get the full import history and finalise the re-flagging before you pay.
  • No cabin: if you think you'll want to sleep aboard, this isn't your boat; look at a sundeck instead.

Licence, taxes and fixed costs

Under 8 metres there's no registration tax on a boat already registered in Spain, and either the PNB or the PER is enough to skipper it. When buying you'll pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% of the price in Catalonia) and the Harbour Master's fee; I cover this in the guide to paperwork and change of ownership. Mooring for a boat this length runs €2,500 to 4,000 a year on the Catalan coast depending on the marina, and considerably less on dry storage; rates are in the mooring guide.

Alternatives I'd consider

The Quicksilver Activ 605 Open (smaller and cheaper, runs on the basic Licence); the Quicksilver Activ 675 Sundeck (if you want a cabin). And if you're not sure which type of boat suits you, the two-minute test will clarify it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Beneteau Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck cost?

It mainly depends on the year, engine and equipment. 2015 to 2017: €26,000 to 36,500; 2018 to 2020: €29,000 to 43,000. These are indicative ranges from European listings in September 2026; the real price is settled by viewing the boat.

What licence do I need to skipper a Beneteau Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck?

Under 8 metres the PNB is enough (up to 5 nm from a shelter), and with the PER you can sail up to 12 nm and to the Balearics with the relevant extension. The Navigation Licence doesn't reach: it's capped at 6 metres.

Is registration tax due when buying a used Beneteau Flyer 6.6 SPACEdeck?

No, if it's already registered in Spain: the tax is paid only once. When you buy it, you do pay transfer tax (ITP, 5% in Catalonia) and the change-of-ownership fee at the Harbour Master's Office.
