I'll remind you before your boat's papers expire

By Juan MoranteChecked on 3 November 2026

The inspection, the insurance or the flares always seem to expire at the worst moment: at the fuel dock, during a check or just as you go to sell the boat. Leave me the date and I will send you an email before it runs out. It is free and you can unsubscribe whenever you like.

How it works

  1. Choose what expires and enter the date (up to four items at once).
  2. I email you about 60 days before, again at 30, at 7 and on the day itself.
  3. If you change boat or have already renewed, just reply to the email and I will update or delete it.

Reminders are a guide: the date that counts is the one in your documents. If you are not sure whether your boat needs the ITB, see the ITB guide.

Add your dates

Data controller: Juan Morante (Plata Marine), juan@platamarine.com. Purpose: sending you reminders for the dates you enter and, only if you tick the second box, Plata Marine news. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any reminder or writing to juan@platamarine.com. Your data is not shared with third parties. Retention: until the last date you entered has passed or you unsubscribe. Rights: access, rectification, erasure, objection and the other GDPR rights; you can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). More in the privacy policy.

Fields marked * are required.

What expires on a boat

What people forget most, with the usual interval. Always check against your own documents:

  • ITB (Spanish boat inspection): every 5 years at most for list 7 boats from 6 to 24 m; for others it depends on the list and length.
  • Third-party liability insurance: when the policy ends, usually every year.
  • Seaworthiness certificate: the validity shown on the certificate itself.
  • Flares, rockets and smoke signals: the expiry date of each unit.
  • Extinguishers, life raft and inflatable lifejackets: the servicing set by the manufacturer and the rules.
  • Boating licence (Spanish card): renewed every 10 years, and every 5 from age 70.

Your data

I only ask for what I need to remind you: an email and the dates. The boat name is optional. I do not use this data for anything else unless you tick the news box, and every reminder tells you how to unsubscribe.
